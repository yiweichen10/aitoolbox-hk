Submit an AI Tool

Submissions are free. Every tool that enters the catalog is tested with a real account by our editors before it gets a review, and the catalog is updated daily. What we cannot offer — at any price — is a better ranking.

How to submit

Email AIToolLabTeam@gmail.com with the subject "Tool submission" and include:

1 · The basics

Tool name, official URL, and a one-line description of what it does.

2 · Pricing page URL

We verify pricing from the official page at review time, so link it directly.

3 · Category suggestion

One category from our category list that fits best. Our editors make the final call.

4 · Access

A trial code or free-tier access if the tool is paid — reviews come from real usage, not demo decks. Tools that cannot be tested cannot be reviewed.

What happens next

Editorial review

The catalog is updated daily; review order follows editorial priority and we cannot promise a date. Tools that cannot be tested are not listed.

Honest scores, no exceptions

Rankings are not for sale — the same rule printed on the homepage. A review may be critical; that is the product. If a fact is wrong, email us and it is fixed within 48 hours.

What we do not accept

Paid placements, link exchanges, "guaranteed review" packages, and AI wrappers with no working product. If your tool ships, we want to see it.
