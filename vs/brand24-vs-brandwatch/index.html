Brand24 vs Brandwatch

Both tools are in our catalog with hands-on reviews: Brand24 scores 4.5/5 and Brandwatch scores 3.5/5. Below is everything our testing surfaced — scores, pricing, strengths and weaknesses — without sponsorship from either side.

Brand24

4.5/5 editor score
From $199/mo annual · Pro $399/mo annual
  • ✓  Source coverage is genuinely wide for the price - 25M+ sources including TikTok, Twitch, Telegram, podcasts, review sites and newsletters, not just the big four social networks
  • ✓  The alerting layer is the part worth paying for: anomaly detection spikes your phone when mentions explode, which is the crisis-catching moment that justifies the bill
  • —  Prices have climbed hard: Individual went from $79 in older reviews to $249 monthly now, and the trajectory suggests more hikes - lock annual rates and expect renewal pain

Brandwatch

3.5/5 editor score
Quote-only · ~$800-$1,500/mo entry
  • ✓  The archive is the moat: 1.4-1.7 trillion conversations back to 2008/2010 across 100M+ sources - when a client asks how sentiment shifted over five years, the data actually exists here
  • ✓  Iris anomaly detection watches your queries and flags spikes and sentiment shifts on its own, so a Friday-night crisis pings you instead of hiding in a dashboard nobody refreshed
  • —  No public pricing, no free trial, no self-serve - buyer reports put entry at ~$800-$1,500/mo, mid-market $2,000-$5,000/mo, enterprise $60K-$150K+/yr, with $5,000-$20,000 implementation fees on top
