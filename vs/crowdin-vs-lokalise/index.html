Crowdin vs Lokalise

Both tools are in our catalog with hands-on reviews: Crowdin scores 4.3/5 and Lokalise scores 3.9/5. Below is everything our testing surfaced — scores, pricing, strengths and weaknesses — without sponsorship from either side.

Crowdin

4.3/5 editor score
Free + Pro from $50/mo · Team $150/mo
  • ✓  Crowdin AI, translation memory, glossaries and QA checks are on every plan including Free, so the free tier is usable for a genuine first project
  • ✓  Hosted-word pricing is the one model you can calculate on a napkin - source words times languages - instead of guessing at seats and credits
  • —  Hosted words multiply by target languages, so a modest app in ten languages can blow past the 60K free allowance in a single project
Full Crowdin review

Lokalise

3.9/5 editor score
Explorer $144/mo · Growth $375/mo
  • ✓  Unlimited hosted words on every paid tier means the bill never multiplies by target languages the way Crowdin and Phrase managed-word plans do
  • ✓  Standard AI and machine translation are unlimited on every plan, so UI strings get a first pass at no extra cost
  • —  No free plan at all - the floor is $144/month, which is steep for a solo founder or a first small job
