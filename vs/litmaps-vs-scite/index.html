Litmaps vs Scite

Both tools are in our catalog with hands-on reviews: Litmaps scores 4.2/5 and Scite scores 4.3/5. Below is everything our testing surfaced — scores, pricing, strengths and weaknesses — without sponsorship from either side.

Litmaps

4.2/5 editor score
Free + Pro $10/mo · $120/yr annual
  • ✓  The map view does something keyword search genuinely cannot - it shows you the papers sitting between your seeds, including ones your query words would never match
  • ✓  New-paper alerts are the real product: set a map for your niche once and relevant work lands in your inbox weekly without re-running searches
  • —  It is a discovery tool, not a systematic one - citation maps miss papers with sparse citations and cannot prove coverage, so it is useless as the primary search for a PRISMA-style systematic review
Full Litmaps review

Scite

4.3/5 editor score
From $12/mo · Pro $40/mo annual
  • ✓  The contrasting-citation filter is genuinely unique - it is the only mainstream tool that tells you who DISAGREES with a paper, and it surfaces disputed claims before you repeat them in something you get paid for
  • ✓  Reference Check on your own draft is a pre-submission smoke detector - it flagged two disputed sources in a client article that had already passed human review
  • —  No free tier worth the name - 7-day trial, then $144/year minimum, while Elicit and Semantic Scholar give away more for $0
