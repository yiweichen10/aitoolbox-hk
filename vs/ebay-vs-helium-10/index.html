eBay vs Helium 10

Both tools are in our catalog with hands-on reviews: eBay scores 4.3/5 and Helium 10 scores 4.3/5. Below is everything our testing surfaced — scores, pricing, strengths and weaknesses — without sponsorship from either side.

E

eBay

4.3/5 editor score
13.6% + $0.40 per sale
  • ✓  Zero barrier to entry: no application, no monthly fee, 250 free listings a month, and the seller app can scan, price and list a thrift-store find in minutes
  • ✓  Demand depth nobody else has for used, vintage and discontinued items - 130M+ buyers who specifically search for the exact model you are holding
  • —  The fee stack is heavier than the headline: FVF applies to shipping AND sales tax, the per-order fixed fee crushes low-ticket items (a $15 sale keeps about $12.50), and international buyers add 1.65%
Full eBay review

Helium 10

4.3/5 editor score
Free plan · Paid from $29/mo
  • ✓  Xray in the Chrome Extension is the single best product-validation tool in e-commerce: hover over any Amazon search results and get real sales estimates, revenue, and review velocity per listing in seconds. One good product decision pays for a year of Platinum
  • ✓  Cerebro reverse-ASIN lookup shows every keyword a competitor product ranks for, with search volume and ranking position. It is the fastest way to build a keyword list for a new listing, and it is a genuine moat over free alternatives
  • —  Price creep is real: Starter was long marketed around $39 and the entry point has shuffled across tiers; Diamond at $229/mo annual is a heavy fixed cost for anyone not yet selling consistently. Monthly billing is 30-40% pricier than the advertised numbers
