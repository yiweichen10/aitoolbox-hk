Deepgram vs Wondercraft AI

Both tools are in our catalog with hands-on reviews: Deepgram scores 4.4/5 and Wondercraft AI scores 4.2/5. Below is everything our testing surfaced — scores, pricing, strengths and weaknesses — without sponsorship from either side.

Deepgram

4.4/5 editor score
Free (45K min/mo) + Pay-as-you-go $0.004/min + Enterprise custom
  • ✓  The pricing is the killer feature. At $0.004/minute, you can resell transcription at $0.50-$2.00/minute and actually make money. Compare that to Rev or Temi where the API cost eats 60-80% of your margin
  • ✓  Sub-100ms streaming latency means you can build real-time captioning apps that do not feel laggy. I have tested it side-by-side with Google Speech-to-Text and Deepgram is consistently 2-3x faster on the same audio
  • —  No built-in editing interface. Deepgram is an API, not a product. You get raw JSON with timestamps and confidence scores. If you want a nice UI for reviewing and editing transcripts, you have to build it yourself or pay for Otter.ai/Descript on top

Wondercraft AI

4.2/5 editor score
Free + Creator from $25/mo
  • ✓  Turns text, PDF, URL, or ideas into a fully produced podcast - script, voices, music, SFX, show notes, publish
  • ✓  Voice cloning trains a client's own host voice from a short sample (1 on Creator, 5 on Pro)
  • —  Free plan is a teaser - 6 credits a month (~6 min audio), not a workflow
