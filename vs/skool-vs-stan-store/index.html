Skool vs Stan Store

Both tools are in our catalog with hands-on reviews: Skool scores 4.7/5 and Stan Store scores 4.7/5. Below is everything our testing surfaced — scores, pricing, strengths and weaknesses — without sponsorship from either side.

Skool

4.7/5 editor score
Pro $99/mo flat - 0% Skool fee (Stripe 2.9% + $0.30); 14-day free trial, no card
  • ✓  Zero platform cut on Pro - you keep 100% of member payments after Stripe processing (~2.9% + $0.30). On a $5,000/month community that saves $400-600 versus Patreon's commission and far more than Whop's 30% marketplace fee.
  • ✓  Flat $99/month no matter the size - 50 members or 50,000 cost the same. No per-seat fees, no tier jumps, so your margin grows as the group grows.
  • —  No native automation, email, or CRM - no welcome DMs, no churn alerts, no tags, no scheduled campaigns. Past ~50-100 paid members you do manual ops or bolt on Zapier/ConvertKit and pay for them.

Stan Store

4.7/5 editor score
From $29/mo (14-day trial)
  • ✓  Zero platform cut - you keep 100% of every sale after Stripe. On a $97 template sold 20 times a month you clear about $1,882; Gumroad's free plan would take roughly $194 of that. The flat fee pays for itself once you pass about $300 a month in sales.
  • ✓  Fast to launch - I had a live, purchase-ready page in under 30 minutes: upload a file, connect Stripe, set a price, share the link. No separate course host, no Calendly, no open Gumroad tab.
  • —  No free plan - you pay $29 a month before your first sale. For a total beginner with no audience yet, that is a real monthly bill with no guaranteed return, and Stan sends you no traffic.
