Carrd vs Webflow AI

Both tools are in our catalog with hands-on reviews: Carrd scores 4.2/5 and Webflow AI scores 4.6/5. Below is everything our testing surfaced — scores, pricing, strengths and weaknesses — without sponsorship from either side.

Carrd

4.2/5 editor score
Free + Pro from $9/yr
  • ✓  $19/year for a custom-domain one-pager with SSL, forms and analytics - under $1.60/month
  • ✓  Zero platform fees: payment forms run your own Stripe keys and Carrd takes no cut
  • —  One page only - no blog, no CMS, no store; outgrowing it means migrating platforms

Webflow AI

4.6/5 editor score
Free + Core from $29/mo
  • ✓  The visual editor is the real deal — I built a 5-page SaaS landing site in 11 hours start to finish, and the client paid $3,500. Without AI assistance, that same project would have taken 25+ hours
  • ✓  Responsive breakpoint editor actually works. You design the desktop version and the AI suggests tablet/mobile adjustments. I catch layout issues in minutes instead of spending an hour manually testing every screen size
  • —  The learning curve is steeper than Webflow wants to admit. The first two weeks felt like being dropped into Photoshop with zero design training — you will watch a lot of YouTube tutorials before you ship anything professional
