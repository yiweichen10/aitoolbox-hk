Lokalise

4.5/5 editor score

  • ✓  Unlimited hosted words on every paid tier means the bill never multiplies by target languages the way Crowdin and Phrase managed-word plans do
  • ✓  Standard AI and machine translation are unlimited on every plan, so UI strings get a first pass at no extra cost
  • —  No free plan at all - the floor is $144/month, which is steep for a solo founder or a first small job

Phrase

4.6/5 editor score
From $27/mo · Team $1,245/mo
  • ✓  Unlimited TMS seats on every paid plan, so adding translators or client reviewers does not raise the bill the way per-seat tools do
  • ✓  Phrase Language AI is a real orchestration layer - AutoSelect picks the best engine per language, Quality Performance Score flags only the segments worth a human eye, and Auto Adapt keeps brand voice
  • —  The floor is $27/month and jumps to $525/month almost immediately if you need more than one TMS seat or any Strings seats - there is no friendly mid-tier for a small solo shop
