Etsy vs Substack

Both tools are in our catalog with hands-on reviews: Etsy scores 4.1/5 and Substack scores 4.2/5. Below is everything our testing surfaced — scores, pricing, strengths and weaknesses — without sponsorship from either side.

Etsy

4.1/5 editor score
$0.20 to list + ~10% per sale
  • ✓  Built-in traffic is the real product - roughly 80 million active buyers searching for things to buy, which no standalone store tool offers
  • ✓  Digital products have zero marginal cost: design once, deliver automatically, sell the same file a thousand times
  • —  The fee stack is heavier than the headline 10% - with Offsite Ads and processing, effective cost is often 20-25% of revenue
Full Etsy review

Substack

4.2/5 editor score
Free to publish + 10% of paid revenue (plus Stripe fees)
  • ✓  Free to publish forever - no monthly fee, no subscriber cap, no feature gate, so you pay nothing until a reader actually pays you
  • ✓  Built-in recommendation network drives 30-50% of new signups for many writers at zero cost - no other newsletter tool gives away discovery like this
  • —  The 10% platform cut never drops - at $10,000/month paid that is $1,000 a month ($12,000 a year) with no volume discount and nothing to negotiate

Which should you choose?

Choose Etsy if your work matches its strengths listed above. Choose Substack if you want to start on a free tier or editor score is your primary tiebreaker or your work matches its strengths listed above.

Questions about Etsy vs Substack

Is Substack better than Etsy?

On our editor scores, Etsy rates 4.1/5 and Substack rates 4.2/5. Scores weigh pricing, output quality and workflow fit from hands-on testing — read both reviews and match the strengths to your own workflow before deciding.

Which is cheaper: Etsy or Substack?

Etsy is listed at "$0.20 to list + ~10% per sale" and Substack at "Free to publish + 10% of paid revenue (plus Stripe fees)" (verified 2026-11-27). Substack has a free tier; Etsy is paid from the start.

Can I use Etsy and Substack together?

Most readers pick one from the AI Income category — running both is possible but usually redundant. If your workflow spans different stages, the pros list above shows where each one is strongest.
