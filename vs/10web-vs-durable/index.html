10Web vs Durable

Both tools are in our catalog with hands-on reviews: 10Web scores 4.3/5 and Durable scores 4.7/5. Below is everything our testing surfaced — scores, pricing, strengths and weaknesses — without sponsorship from either side.

10Web

4.3/5 editor score
From $10/mo · Agency $42.5/mo
  • ✓  The site is real WordPress, not a locked format - you can export it and move hosts, which is not true of most AI builders
  • ✓  White-label dashboard, branded builder and Stripe/PayPal billing on Agency Core make reselling a genuine product business rather than an affiliate link
  • —  The generated output is a first draft - copy is generic and layouts need editing, so 'a site in five minutes' is a sales line, not a delivery plan
Full 10Web review

Durable

4.7/5 editor score
Free + Launch from $25/mo
  • ✓  It genuinely ships a working site in under a minute. Type what you do and it returns a structured page with copy, layout, and images. For freelancers selling local-business websites, that is a client deliverable you can show before the meeting ends.
  • ✓  The bundle is the real value: CRM, booking, invoicing, and an AI assistant that answers business questions are included in one subscription. You replace six tools (site builder, CRM, calendar, review tool, ad maker, copywriter) with one bill.
  • —  Design control is shallow. You cannot edit raw HTML, and the layouts follow Durable's templates. If a client wants a custom, brand-heavy site, you will hit a wall fast - Wix or Webflow give you more room.
