Manus vs Voiceflow

Both tools are in our catalog with hands-on reviews: Manus scores 4.6/5 and Voiceflow scores 3.5/5. Below is everything our testing surfaced — scores, pricing, strengths and weaknesses — without sponsorship from either side.

Manus

4.6/5 editor score
Free credits + Starter from $20/mo
  • ✓  It finishes whole jobs, not steps. Most AI tools hand you a draft and leave the last mile to you. Manus keeps working - it plans, opens a browser, runs code, checks its own output, and returns a finished PDF, deck, or website. For reselling deliverables, that gap between 'draft' and 'done' is the whole business.
  • ✓  The virtual computer is real. Each task spins up a Linux sandbox with a browser, terminal, and file system, so it can log into sites, scrape many pages in sequence, run Python on your CSV, and build charts - things a chat model simply cannot do on its own.
  • —  Credits vanish and mistakes cost you. Monthly and daily credits do not roll over, and when the agent misplans a task, its retries and self-corrections burn credits too. A single complex research-plus-website job can eat a big chunk of a Starter allowance before you get a usable result.

Voiceflow

3.5/5 editor score
Free + Pro from $60/mo
  • ✓  The visual canvas is genuinely best in class - conversation design feels like Figma, and a non-technical client can follow what you built without a walkthrough
  • ✓  The hybrid flow design (hand-drawn happy path plus AI fallback node) means the parts that must be deterministic stay deterministic and everything off-script is handled by the model
  • —  Credits are the hidden tax - roughly one per chat message and about ten per minute of voice, they do not roll over, and when they run out mid-month your agent stops responding with no top-up option
