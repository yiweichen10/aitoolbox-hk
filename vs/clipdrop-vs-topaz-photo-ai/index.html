Clipdrop vs Topaz Photo AI

Both tools are in our catalog with hands-on reviews: Clipdrop scores 4.2/5 and Topaz Photo AI scores 4.3/5. Below is everything our testing surfaced — scores, pricing, strengths and weaknesses — without sponsorship from either side.

Clipdrop

4.2/5 editor score
Free $0 + Pro $9/mo (some listings show $15; API from ~$49/mo via Jasper)
  • ✓  Background removal handles hair, glass, and fiddly edges about as well as Remove.bg, but it lives in the same dashboard as eight other tools
  • ✓  Relight is the one I keep: change the lighting on a product shot after the shoot, no reshoot - nothing else does this at the price
  • —  Even Pro caps at 1,000 images per 24 hours per tool, so a big catalog refresh still hits a wall and pushes you to Jasper enterprise pricing
