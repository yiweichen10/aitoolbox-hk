Fiverr vs Substack

Both tools are in our catalog with hands-on reviews: Fiverr scores 4.2/5 and Substack scores 4.2/5. Below is everything our testing surfaced — scores, pricing, strengths and weaknesses — without sponsorship from either side.

Fiverr

4.2/5 editor score
Free to sell · 20% per sale
  • ✓  Zero cost to start - you list up to 4 gigs free and pay nothing until money comes in, which makes it the cheapest real-world test of whether anyone pays for your skills
  • ✓  Demand already exists: buyers arrive with a task and a budget, so a well-positioned gig can produce a first sale within days rather than the months a personal site needs
  • —  The 20% cut is flat and non-negotiable - it applies to tips and extras too, and unlike Upwork there is no lower tier no matter how much you earn
Full Fiverr review

Substack

4.2/5 editor score
Free to publish + 10% of paid revenue (plus Stripe fees)
  • ✓  Free to publish forever - no monthly fee, no subscriber cap, no feature gate, so you pay nothing until a reader actually pays you
  • ✓  Built-in recommendation network drives 30-50% of new signups for many writers at zero cost - no other newsletter tool gives away discovery like this
  • —  The 10% platform cut never drops - at $10,000/month paid that is $1,000 a month ($12,000 a year) with no volume discount and nothing to negotiate
