Ghost vs Stripe

Both tools are in our catalog with hands-on reviews: Ghost scores 4.6/5 and Stripe scores 4.7/5. Below is everything our testing surfaced — scores, pricing, strengths and weaknesses — without sponsorship from either side.

G

Ghost

4.6/5 editor score
From $29/mo · 0% of revenue
  • ✓  0% platform fee on subscription revenue is permanent and written into every paid plan - at $2,000/mo MRR that saves roughly $2,400/yr versus Substack's 10% cut
  • ✓  Open source under MIT and run by a non-profit foundation: you can export everything, self-host the same software, and the product cannot be sold or shut down under you
  • —  There is no free plan, and the $15 Starter tier cannot take payments at all - a paid newsletter starts at $29/mo (Publisher, yearly billing)
Full Ghost review
S

Stripe

4.7/5 editor score
2.9% + 30¢ per card charge
  • ✓  No monthly fee, no platform commission and no lock-in: 2.9% + 30c per successful charge is the whole bill for taking a card payment, and a Payment Link needs zero code
  • ✓  Invoicing at 0.4% (capped at $2 per invoice) is the cheapest serious way for freelancers and consultants to bill clients and get paid on time
  • —  The 2.9% + 30c headline is US domestic cards only - international cards and currency conversion push real costs toward 4.4-6% on cross-border work
