Etsy vs Patreon

Both tools are in our catalog with hands-on reviews: Etsy scores 4.1/5 and Patreon scores 4.2/5. Below is everything our testing surfaced — scores, pricing, strengths and weaknesses — without sponsorship from either side.

Etsy

4.1/5 editor score
$0.20 to list + ~10% per sale
  • ✓  Built-in traffic is the real product - roughly 80 million active buyers searching for things to buy, which no standalone store tool offers
  • ✓  Digital products have zero marginal cost: design once, deliver automatically, sell the same file a thousand times
  • —  The fee stack is heavier than the headline 10% - with Offsite Ads and processing, effective cost is often 20-25% of revenue
Full Etsy review

Patreon

4.2/5 editor score
Free + 10% platform fee (legacy plans 5-12%)
  • ✓  No monthly fee to open a page - you pay a percentage of what you earn, so launching a membership costs nothing upfront
  • ✓  Recurring revenue is the whole point - patrons auto-charge every month, which beats one-off sales for income stability
  • —  The fee stack is real - 10% platform cut plus about 2.9% + $0.30 processing, so a $10 patron leaves you roughly $8.40; legacy plans ranged 5-12% and the 12% tier is the highest of any major creator platform

Questions about Etsy vs Patreon

On our editor scores, Etsy rates 4.1/5 and Patreon rates 4.2/5. Scores weigh pricing, output quality and workflow fit from hands-on testing — read both reviews and match the strengths to your own workflow before deciding.

Etsy is listed at "$0.20 to list + ~10% per sale" and Patreon at "Free + 10% platform fee (legacy plans 5-12%)" (verified 2026-11-24). Patreon has a free tier; Etsy is paid from the start.

Most readers pick one from the AI Income category — running both is possible but usually redundant. If your workflow spans different stages, the pros list above shows where each one is strongest.
