Ghost vs Patreon

Both tools are in our catalog with hands-on reviews: Ghost scores 4.6/5 and Patreon scores 4.2/5. Below is everything our testing surfaced — scores, pricing, strengths and weaknesses — without sponsorship from either side.

G

Ghost

4.6/5 editor score
From $29/mo · 0% of revenue
  • ✓  0% platform fee on subscription revenue is permanent and written into every paid plan - at $2,000/mo MRR that saves roughly $2,400/yr versus Substack's 10% cut
  • ✓  Open source under MIT and run by a non-profit foundation: you can export everything, self-host the same software, and the product cannot be sold or shut down under you
  • —  There is no free plan, and the $15 Starter tier cannot take payments at all - a paid newsletter starts at $29/mo (Publisher, yearly billing)
Full Ghost review

Patreon

4.2/5 editor score
Free + 10% platform fee (legacy plans 5-12%)
  • ✓  No monthly fee to open a page - you pay a percentage of what you earn, so launching a membership costs nothing upfront
  • ✓  Recurring revenue is the whole point - patrons auto-charge every month, which beats one-off sales for income stability
  • —  The fee stack is real - 10% platform cut plus about 2.9% + $0.30 processing, so a $10 patron leaves you roughly $8.40; legacy plans ranged 5-12% and the 12% tier is the highest of any major creator platform
