Clay vs Semrush AI

Both tools are in our catalog with hands-on reviews: Clay scores 4.7/5 and Semrush AI scores 4.7/5. Below is everything our testing surfaced — scores, pricing, strengths and weaknesses — without sponsorship from either side.

Clay

4.7/5 editor score
Free + Launch $167/mo · Growth $446/mo
  • ✓  The waterfall is the whole point and it works: for any field you want - a work email, a mobile number, a company's revenue band - Clay tries several providers in order and stops at the first hit, so you stop paying for five enrichment subscriptions and stop stitching CSVs together by hand
  • ✓  The money math is easy to defend. Launch is $167 a month, and a single lead-list or outbound job billed at $800-$3,000 pays for the subscription several times over - this is one of the few AI tools where a freelancer can name the client who covers the cost
  • —  It gets expensive fast and the plan price hides it. Every enrichment is metered, top-ups carry a 30% premium, and there is no spend cap unless you build one - teams regularly discover their real monthly bill is two to four times the tier sticker after one oversized list run

Semrush AI

4.7/5 editor score
Free + Pro from $139.95/mo
  • ✓  Largest keyword database in the industry. When you search for a keyword, you actually get useful data — search volume trends, difficulty scores, intent classification — not just vague estimates
  • ✓  Competitor analysis is the real money maker. Drop in any domain and see their top pages, traffic sources, backlink profile, and ad spend. This alone justifies the subscription for anyone doing client SEO work
  • —  Expensive, and it keeps getting pricier. The Pro plan jumped from $119.95 to $139.95. For solo freelancers or small blogs, this is a significant monthly expense that needs to pay for itself fast
