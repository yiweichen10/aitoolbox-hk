Privacy Policy

The short version: AI Tool Lab has no accounts and no logins. We measure aggregate traffic, you stay anonymous, and we never sell data. Last updated 2026-11-26.

What we collect

Traffic analytics (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics to measure aggregate traffic — page views, referrers, country and device type. This data is not used to identify individuals. You can block it with any ad blocker or browser setting.

Tool likes (localStorage + anonymous counter)

The like button stores a random token and the slugs you liked in your browser's localStorage. The server stores only the per-tool count and an anonymous token — no name, no email, no IP profile. IP addresses are stored transiently (24 hours) solely to prevent abuse, then expire.

Site preferences (localStorage)

Dark-mode preference is stored locally in your browser. It never leaves your device.

Email you send us

If you email us (corrections, submissions), we read and keep the email to fix the issue. We do not add you to any mailing list.

Affiliate links

Some outbound links to tool websites are affiliate links, marked with rel="nofollow sponsored". If you buy through one, we may earn a commission at no cost to you. Commissions fund our testing subscriptions but never influence scores, rankings or verdicts — the scoring rubric is public and contains no manual overrides.

What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not run accounts or logins, so we hold no personal profiles.
  • We do not send marketing email.

Your choices

Clear your browser's localStorage to erase every preference and like token we store. Block Google Analytics with any standard blocker. To ask anything about this policy, email AIToolLabTeam@gmail.com.
