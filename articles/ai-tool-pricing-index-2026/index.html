AI Tool Pricing Index 2026: What 261 Tools Actually Cost

2026-11-17 · AI Tool Lab Editorial Team · 11 min read
TL;DR

We analyzed the published price of 261 AI tools. 208 offer a free tier; the median paid plan is $19.99/mo. Original data, reproducible method.

The price on an AI tool's homepage is rarely the price you pay. A "$20/mo" plan can mean a seat, a usage bucket, or an annual rate divided to look smaller. We pulled the published monthly price of every tool in our database to see what the market actually charges — and how much of it is free.

This is not a list of our favorite tools. It is a pricing dataset you can quote, with the method written down so you can check it. If you only want the short version: 208 of 261 tools (80%) advertise a free tier, and among the 173 tools with a clearly published USD monthly price, the median entry plan is $19.99 per month.

The headline numbers

We reviewed 261 tools across the categories we track. Here is what the pricing labels actually say:

  • 208 tools (80%) advertise a free tier. That includes limited free plans, open-source and self-hosted options, and free credit allowances. It does not guarantee the free tier is useful for real work.
  • 173 tools (66%) publish a clear USD monthly price we could extract and verify from their listed plan.
  • 88 tools (34%) do not publish a comparable monthly figure. These are API/usage-based tools, per-seat or per-builder plans, one-time purchases, non-USD pricing, or free-only products. We left them out of the averages rather than guess.
  • Median entry price: $19.99/mo. Mean: $31.20/mo. The gap between the two is the story — a handful of expensive suites pull the average up, while the typical tool sits near $19.99.

What the spread tells buyers

The cheapest entry points cluster in AI Audio ($17.99 median), AI Automation ($19) and AI Search ($20). These are the easiest categories to trial without a budget conversation.

The expensive end is AI SEO ($95 median), AI Marketing ($47) and AI Translation ($38.50). In AI SEO and marketing, suites bundle several jobs into one subscription, so a higher sticker price can still replace multiple smaller tools. That is a workflow decision, not a price comparison.

Two patterns are worth flagging. First, "free" is now the default entry point for most categories — the question is what the free tier lets you do before it becomes a paywall. Second, a low entry price often hides the real cost: per-seat billing, credit top-ups, or an API key you pay for separately. The $19.99 median is a starting point for a budget, not the total cost of ownership.

How we calculated this (methodology)

We extracted prices from the published plan labels in our tool database on 2026-11-17. The rules were deliberately conservative:

  • Entry price is the lowest explicitly stated USD per-month figure in a tool's plan label — for example "$29 - $249/mo" contributes $29, not $249.
  • Annual-equivalent prices (e.g. "$4.17/mo when billed yearly") are excluded, because they are not the month-to-month price.
  • Per-seat, per-user, per-builder, per-token and per-sale figures are excluded, because they are not comparable to a solo monthly plan.
  • Non-USD prices, one-time purchases and usage-only pricing are counted as "no published monthly price" rather than converted or estimated.
  • Free tier means the label advertises any form of free access, including limited plans, free credits, open-source and self-hosted options.

Prices change often and plans vary by region and billing terms. Treat every figure as a snapshot, and confirm the current price on the vendor's own site before you buy.

Frequently asked questions

What is the average cost of an AI tool? Across the 173 tools with a published USD monthly price, the mean entry plan is $31.20/mo and the median is $19.99/mo. The median is the more useful number: the average is pulled upward by a small group of premium suites.

What percentage of AI tools have a free tier? 208 of 261 tools (80%) advertise some form of free access. A free tier is best treated as a way to test the workflow, not as a guarantee that the tool can run your workload at no cost.

Which category is most expensive? In our data, AI SEO has the highest median entry price at $95/mo, followed by AI Marketing at $47, though each is based on a small number of priced tools.

Why are some tools missing from the averages? 88 tools do not publish a monthly USD figure we can compare — they charge by usage, per seat, by commission, or only quote enterprise rates. We exclude them rather than invent a number. Their pricing may still be excellent value for the right buyer.
