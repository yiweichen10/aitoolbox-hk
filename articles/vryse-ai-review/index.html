Troubleshooting Common Vryse Issues

My GEO score is stuck at 40. Why?

Usually, this is because your writing is too flowery. LLMs hate adverbs. They love nouns and numbers. Try removing words like "definitely," "actually," and "literally." Replace them with "In a study of 500 users..." or "According to the 2026 Bureau of AI Statistics..."

Vryse isn't finding any citations for my brand.

It takes time for AI models to crawl the web or for their "Search" modes to index your new content. Ensure you have submitted your sitemap to Vryse’s "Priority Indexer." This doesn't guarantee a crawl, but it puts you in the queue.

The tool is slow on my 5,000-word guide.

Vryse runs a mini-LLM simulation for every paragraph. For long-form content, we recommend breaking the article into 1,000-word chunks and auditing them separately. This keeps the processing time under 30 seconds.

Deep Dive: The Science of RAG and Why Vryse Matters

To understand why Vryse is necessary, you have to understand Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). When you ask an AI a question, it doesn't just "know" the answer. It searches its database (or the web) for "chunks" of text that are relevant.

Vryse helps you format your content so it is "Easy to Chunk."

  • Header Structure: It suggests H2s that map to common query intents.
  • Sentence Length: It encourages shorter, fact-dense sentences that are easier for vector databases to index.
  • Metadata: It helps you inject schema that AI crawlers prioritize.

By optimizing for RAG, you are essentially making it "low-cost" for the AI to include you. Algorithms are lazy; they will always pick the source that is easiest to process and most likely to be correct. Vryse makes you that source.
