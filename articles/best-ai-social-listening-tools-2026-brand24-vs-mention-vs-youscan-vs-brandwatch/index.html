Best AI Social Listening Tools 2026 — Brand24 vs Mention vs YouScan vs Brandwatch

2026-09-20 · AI Social Media · AI Tool Lab Editorial Team · 📖 36 min read
⚡ TL;DR
Brand24, Mention, YouScan and Brandwatch compared on real 2026 pricing, AI sentiment accuracy and hidden costs. Which social listening tool fits your budget — and which one will waste it.

Grand View Research values the social media listening market at $9.2 billion in 2024, projects $11.7 billion in 2026, and puts it at $20.2 billion by 2030 at a 14.3% CAGR. Demand is booming. Pricing just collapsed upward: Mention, the category's most famous budget tool, killed its $49/month Solo plan in July 2025 and now sells a single Company plan from $599/month on an annual contract. That is a 12x jump at the door, while most comparison articles still list $49 as the entry price. Pick a tool from stale data and you will either budget for a product that no longer exists or overpay by thousands for features you will never open.

I checked official pricing pages, acquisition records and independent benchmarks on the four AI social listening tools that matter in 2026 — Brand24, Mention, YouScan and Brandwatch. This is what the market actually looks like, including one acquisition through a French commercial court that explains why your favorite cheap tool disappeared.

Why cheap social listening died: the Mention story

Mention launched in Paris in 2012 and spent a decade as the default recommendation for "affordable brand monitoring." Behind the scenes it was bleeding: the company entered court-ordered receivership in France, and in May 2024 Agorapulse, a profitable, bootstrapped social media management company, bought the assets for a transfer plan of roughly €4.67 million. Mention had around 2,000 customers and 80% of revenue from outside France, but never made low-priced listening profitable.

Emeric Ernoult, Agorapulse's CEO, was blunt about why: the cost of underlying social data multiplied roughly fiftyfold in five years as platform APIs like X and Reddit moved to expensive firehose pricing. A $49 plan including 5,000 tracked mentions stops penciling out when your data bill scales faster than your subscriber base.

Agorapulse kept legacy Mention customers on their old plans, but in July 2025 it closed Solo ($49), Pro ($99) and Pro Plus ($179) to new buyers. Since January 2026, Mention has also stripped out its Publish and Respond modules — it is monitoring-only. New customers get one option: the Company plan from $599/month billed annually, with 50,000 mentions per month, unlimited users, and extra alerts and two-year historical data sold separately through sales.

The lesson generalizes: every "cheap social listening tool" recommended in a 2024 blog post is repriced, restructured, or dead. Verify against the official pricing page before you budget anything — the same warning applies to Brand24, more on that below.

The best AI social listening tools in 2026: the shortlist

Here is the honest version:

  • Brand24 — the only mainstream platform that still sells self-serve plans a small business can afford. Best value between $199 and $599/month. Its January 2026 MCP integration and LLM Monitoring tab are ahead of the category.
  • Mention — excellent Boolean search and review-site coverage, but it left the budget segment. Compare it against Brandwatch now, not against cheap tools.
  • YouScan — the visual analysis specialist. If your brand lives in user-generated photos (food, drinks, fashion, events), its logo and scene detection finds mentions no text query ever will.
  • Brandwatch — the enterprise research engine under Cision. Over a trillion historical records and the deepest analytics in the market, at roughly $800/month entry and $3,000+ for real deployments.

Brand24: the last affordable full-featured option — and where its pricing traps are

Brand24 pricing starts at $249/month for Individual (or $199/month billed annually): 3 keywords, 2,000 mentions per month, one user, updates every 12 hours. Team is $349 monthly / $299 annual with 7 keywords, 10,000 mentions and unlimited users — the practical starting point for most companies. Pro is $499 / $399 with 12 keywords, 40,000 mentions, real-time updates, AI Events Detection and the AI Brand Assistant. Business is $699 / $599 with 25 keywords and 100,000 mentions. Enterprise starts at $1,499/month, billed annually only. All tiers include a 14-day free trial without a credit card and a 30-day money-back guarantee.

Now the trap. Several 2026 comparison sites still list Brand24 at $79/month entry or $149 for Team — numbers from an older pricing generation. A price tracker that has monitored brand24.com since June 2026 confirms the tiers above with no recorded changes. If your budget model says $948/year and the invoice says $2,388, this is why.

What you get for the money is the strongest AI layer in the mid-market: over 25 million sources across 108 languages, sentiment and emotion classification, and AI-generated topic summaries. Two 2026 releases matter. The MCP integration (January 2026) lets you query monitoring data from Claude, ChatGPT or any MCP-capable agent — ask "why did negative mentions spike Tuesday" in plain language. The LLM Monitoring tab tracks how ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews represent your brand. As search shifts to AI answers, that is reputation monitoring for a channel nobody else covers, included rather than an add-on.

Weak points: sentiment scoring weakens on sarcasm and non-English content — independent testing rated it usable but not hands-off. Historical data runs about a year, shallow next to Brandwatch. Keyword caps are tight, and there is no way to pause an account, which matters for seasonal businesses.

Mention: great engine, new price class

Mention pricing is now a single published option: the Company plan from $599/month on an annual contract — about $7,188/year — including 50,000 monthly mentions, unlimited users and social accounts, monitoring across roughly a billion web and social sources, and review tracking across 75+ sites including Trustpilot, Google and Glassdoor. The base plan includes 5 alerts (tracked topics); more alerts, extra mention quota and two-year historical access are negotiated with sales. The legacy Solo, Pro and Pro Plus plans remain only for existing customers who cannot change them.

The engine is legitimately good. Mention's Boolean query builder handles up to 2,000 characters — express "our brand name minus the unrelated band minus retweets" in one query instead of three overlapping alerts. Sentiment runs in 40+ languages, and share-of-voice benchmarking is clean. For a PR team that needs crisis alerting across news, social and reviews at once, it competes with Brandwatch at a lower entry point.

The structural problem is what it no longer is. Since January 2026 there is no publishing or response functionality — pair it with a scheduler. There is no self-serve signup, and the $49 tool from older articles was discontinued for new buyers in July 2025. For basic monitoring, Brand24 Team covers roughly the same use cases at $299/month annual — one-third of Mention's floor. At enterprise scale, negotiate Mention against Brandwatch and Meltwater and make them fight for the alert count and historical depth.

YouScan: pay for what text queries cannot see

YouScan pricing has two tiers. Starter 3 costs $499/month billed annually: 3 text-based topics, up to 15,000 monthly mentions, unlimited users, and the Insights Copilot capped at 10 questions per month. The Unlimited tier is quote-only, unlocking unlimited topics and the two capabilities YouScan is famous for: Visual Insights and Audience Insights. Careful with third-party data — several aggregators still list legacy tiers at $1,000–$3,000/month that the official page no longer shows.

Visual Insights is the reason to consider YouScan at all. Its image recognition detects brand logos, product shapes and scenes inside user-generated photos — your bottle at a party, your sneaker in a street-style post — with no text mention required. Text-based brand monitoring software structurally cannot find those mentions. For consumer brands whose real exposure is visual (beverages, snacks, cosmetics, apparel, events), that is not a feature, it is the entire buy. Independent reviews rate its visual analysis as industry-leading, and the company (founded by a Ukrainian team, now based in Nicosia, Cyprus) scores strongly on usability.

Trade-offs: the entry plan limits you to 3 topics and 15,000 mentions, and the Copilot's 10-questions-per-month cap is stingy for a tool marketed on conversational intelligence. There is no recorded free trial. And if your mentions are overwhelmingly text (B2B software, finance, news-driven categories), you are paying a premium for a capability you will rarely fire — Brand24 or Mention fit better.

Brandwatch: the research department, not a tool

Brandwatch pricing is not published. Based on dated industry trackers and buyer reports, entry configurations run about $800–$1,000/month, mid-tier deployments $1,500–$2,500, and full enterprise contracts $3,000–$5,000+ per month — annual, usually paid upfront, no free trial, no self-serve path. Cision acquired Brandwatch in 2021, and the platform now connects into PR Newswire distribution and Cision's media database.

What justifies the number: over 100 million sources, a historical archive in the trillions of mentions reaching back to 2010, and Iris — an AI layer that surfaces anomalous spikes, sentiment shifts and emerging themes without you writing the query. Audience analysis goes beyond mention counting into demographic segmentation. For a global brand running always-on consumer research, nothing in the mid-market substitutes for it.

What the sales call will not emphasize: implementation fees that can add $5,000–$20,000, custom report add-ons around $500/month, a Boolean builder with a learning curve measured in weeks, and typically non-cancellable contracts. A five-person team at the entry tier is realistically looking at $9,600–$12,000 in year one. If your actual question is "what are people saying about us," that budget buys an enormous amount of Brand24 Business ($599/month annual, 100,000 mentions) plus a part-time analyst.

What you actually pay per mention

Unit economics expose who each tool is for. Brand24 Team at $299/month annual for 10,000 mentions is about $0.030 per mention. Mention's $599 for 50,000 is roughly $0.012 — the cheapest raw rate here, but behind a $7,188 annual floor. YouScan Starter 3 is about $0.033. Brandwatch at $800 for ~10,000 is $0.08 — the worst rate — but its real product is historical research, not volume. Past the entry tier, per-mention cost converges; what separates buyers is the annual floor. A $0.012 rate is still more expensive than a $0.030 one if it forces 5x the volume. Budget by floor price, not rate card.

AI sentiment analysis: the honest accuracy picture

Every vendor demos AI sentiment analysis as if it were solved. It is not. Automated sentiment on clear, literal English is broadly reliable; accuracy degrades on sarcasm, irony, mixed-language posts and slang. Independent testing of Brand24 found exactly that pattern, and no vendor publishes per-language accuracy — which itself tells you something. YouScan sidesteps part of it with aspect-based sentiment (scoring the attribute discussed — price, taste, packaging — rather than the post overall), which beats a single positive/negative flag.

Practical guidance: treat automated sentiment as a triage layer, not a verdict. Alert on negative mentions and have a human read them before you respond publicly. Any vendor claiming fully hands-off sentiment accuracy in 2026 is selling you a demo, not a workflow.

How to choose without overbuying

Solo operators and small brands: the best social listening tools for small business budgets are Brand24 Individual ($199/month annual) or, below that, genuinely free social listening tools — Google Alerts, platform-native notifications, X/Twitter search saves. They are limited but free, and they catch most direct @-mentions. Upgrade when you need competitor tracking or Reddit/forum/review coverage that alerts miss. Awario at $29/month is worth testing if Brand24's floor is still high.

Agencies: the deciding features for social listening tools for agencies are unlimited users, white-label reporting and per-client topic separation. Brand24 Pro and Business include unlimited users and white-label reports; Mention's unlimited-user model works at the $599 floor if you manage fewer, larger clients. Compute cost per client before committing — a $399/month Pro plan across eight clients beats eight $199 Individual plans by a wide margin.

PR and communications teams: prioritize alert latency, review-site coverage and crisis detection — Mention's real-time alerting across news, social and 75+ review sites fits, as do Brand24's real-time tiers. If response workflow matters, remember Mention dropped Respond in January 2026.

Enterprise research: Brandwatch, with Sprinklr and Meltwater as contenders. Negotiate implementation fees, historical backfill depth and alert counts — list prices there are starting points, not prices.

One cross-check before you buy: if your research workflow lives in interview transcripts and surveys rather than public chatter, the AI user research tools comparison covers platforms built for that job, with different price logic. If your need is scheduling and publishing rather than hearing what people say, the AI social media management tools comparison is the right shortlist — Brand24, Mention and YouScan do not post content for you, and bundling confusion is a common way teams end up paying twice.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do AI social listening tools cost in 2026?

Entry prices range from $199/month (Brand24 Individual, annual billing) through $499 (YouScan Starter 3) and $599 (Mention Company, annual only) to roughly $800+ for Brandwatch's cheapest custom configurations. Budget tier effectively starts at Brand24; Awario sits below it at $29/month with lighter coverage. Enterprise deployments of Brandwatch or Meltwater commonly land between $30,000 and $100,000 per year after add-ons.

Is Brand24 better than Mention in 2026?

Brand24 vs Mention now splits by budget class. Below $600/month, Brand24 wins by default — Mention has no self-serve option under its $599 Company floor. At or above that floor, Mention's 50,000 mentions and 2,000-character Boolean queries beat Brand24 Pro on raw capacity and query precision, while Brand24 counters with the MCP integration, LLM Monitoring, real-time updates from its Pro tier up, and a lower commitment. If you need review-site coverage at scale and unlimited users, shortlist Mention; if you need AI-answer visibility and price flexibility, Brand24.

What is the best free social listening tool?

There is no capable free tier left among the major platforms — Brand24, Mention, YouScan and Brandwatch all gate trials or demos. The realistic free stack is Google Alerts (web and news mentions), X/Twitter saved searches, LinkedIn and Reddit native notifications, and review-platform alert emails. That stack catches direct mentions but misses keyword-level discovery, sentiment and competitor benchmarking — a stopgap for validation, not a monitoring practice.

Do I need Brandwatch, or is Brand24 enough?

If your questions are "what is being said, where, and how is sentiment moving," Brand24 Business at $599/month annual answers them with 100,000 mentions and a year of history. Brandwatch earns its $10,000+ annual cost only when you need multi-year historical research, demographic audience analysis, and analyst-grade dashboards as an ongoing function. A useful test: if nobody on your team will spend more than two hours per week inside the platform, you do not need Brandwatch.

Bottom line

The 2026 AI social listening tools market rewards buyers who verify prices and punishes those who copy 2024 recommendations: Mention raised its entry floor twelve-fold to $599/month and went monitoring-only, YouScan repriced to a $499 annual-billing floor, and half the comparison articles in the results still quote numbers that expired in 2024. For most teams, Brand24 is the rational default — real AI capabilities including LLM visibility monitoring, a 14-day no-card trial, and tiers from $199 to $599 monthly. Choose YouScan only if visual UGC detection is your core need, Mention if you are a PR team with an enterprise-adjacent budget, and Brandwatch when social research is a department, not a task. Get the trial before the demo, and never sign a non-cancellable annual contract on a platform your team has not used for a full week.
