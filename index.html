Frase

Verified

Frase is an AI-powered SEO content optimization platform that helps you write articles Google actually wants to rank. Built around SERP analysis and content scoring, it shows you exactly what topics your competitors cover and where your content falls short. I have been using it for client SEO work since late 2024, and the part that makes money is simple: when a client asks "why is my competitor ranking above me?", Frase shows you the answer in 30 seconds instead of 3 hours of manual SERP analysis.

4.5 Free trial available; Solo $14.99/mo, Basic $44.99/mo, Team $114.99/mo
