Polar

Verified

Polar is a merchant of record and billing stack built for developers selling software, SaaS, and AI products. It acts as the legal seller on every receipt, so it collects and remits VAT, GST, and sales tax across 100+ markets while you keep building. For an indie maker shipping a token-metered API or a small SaaS, it turns a checkout link into a global storefront without a tax lawyer.

Smartling

Trending

Smartling is the enterprise localization platform that bundles AI translation with managed translation services and certified project managers - no list prices, contracts median $60K/year per Vendr data. For marketing-heavy teams shipping into 10+ languages it replaces both a TMS and an agency. Freelancers earn by running client Smartling instances as localization-ops consultants.

3.8 Free Core tier · quote-based (~$60K/yr median)

Cline

Trending

Open-source AI coding agent that runs as a VS Code extension. It does not just suggest code — it edits files, runs terminal commands, fixes bugs, and reads your entire project. Free tier with Claude Sonnet 3.5, bring-your-own-API-key model for everything else. If you do freelance dev work and need to ship faster without paying Cursor's monthly fee, this is worth a serious look.

4.0 Free (bring your own API key) + Pro $10/mo

Activepieces

Verified

Open-source no-code automation platform. You build workflows that connect 200+ apps — Slack, GitHub, Google Sheets, Stripe, Notion — without writing code. The self-hostable version means your client data stays on your own server, not on a SaaS platform that might triple its prices next year. If you sell automation services to businesses, Activepieces is how you keep margins high while undercutting Zapier.

3.8 Free (self-hosted) + Cloud $19/mo + Enterprise custom

Frase

Verified

Frase is an AI-powered SEO content optimization platform that helps you write articles Google actually wants to rank. Built around SERP analysis and content scoring, it shows you exactly what topics your competitors cover and where your content falls short. The big differentiator from generic AI writers is the content brief generator — it scans the top 20 ranking pages for your target keyword and builds a data-backed outline with word count targets, topic clusters, and question suggestions pulled from People Also Ask. I have been using it for client SEO work since late 2024, and the part that makes money is simple: when a client asks "why is my competitor ranking above me?", Frase shows you the answer in 30 seconds instead of 3 hours of manual SERP analysis.

4.2 Free trial available; Solo $14.99/mo, Basic $44.99/mo, Team $114.99/mo

Upload a sketch, write a text prompt, and it generates a usable 3D asset in seconds. The money angle: freelancers and small studios use it to offer 3D web experiences, AR filters, and game-ready assets to clients who'd normally pay $500-$2,000 per model. You do it in an afternoon instead of a week.
