You.com

You.com is a privacy-focused AI search engine combining web search with AI chat features like YouChat, YouWrite, and YouCode. A free alternative to Google and ChatGPT.

Consensus is an AI-powered academic search engine that extracts evidence from scientific papers. Ideal for researchers and students seeking credible answers fast.

Perplexity AI

Perplexity AI is a conversational search engine that delivers accurate, real-time answers with direct citations. It effectively replaces traditional search for research and fact-finding.

3.3 Free + Pro $20/mo

Glean

Enterprise AI search platform that unifies knowledge across Slack, Google Drive, Jira, Salesforce, and 100+ apps into one search bar. The real money is not in using it — it is in deploying it for other companies. A single Glean implementation consulting gig runs $5,000-$15,000, and most companies with 50+ employees desperately need someone who knows how to set it up properly.

4.4 Custom enterprise pricing (~$15-$30/seat/mo)

Exa AI

Verified

Exa AI (formerly Metaphor) is an API-first web search engine built for AI agents and LLMs - it matches meaning with embeddings instead of keywords, so long natural-language queries like 'startups quietly building AI tools for construction project managers' come back with the right pages instead of a keyword soup. I build research agents and sales-intelligence tools for clients on it: the tool costs about $0.007 per search, and a client research retainer bills at $500-$2,000 a month, so the margin is close to the full invoice. Cursor uses Exa to ground its coding assistant in fresh docs; you can do the same kind of thing for any vertical you can name.

3.6 Pay-as-you-go from $7/1K req

Genspark

Trending

Genspark is an AI agent workspace that started as an ad-free search engine that built a custom Sparkpage for every query, then pivoted hard into autonomous agents. Its Super Agent splits a request into sub-tasks, coordinates nine-plus foundation models and 80-plus built-in tools, and hands you a finished deliverable instead of an answer: a cited research page in under 15 seconds, a slide deck in about four minutes, a spreadsheet, a website, or even a real phone call made on your behalf. Backed by roughly $385M at a $1.6B valuation (April 2026) with ARR past $200M, it is the fastest-growing AI search/agent hybrid around. The money angle is genuinely broad: sell AI-search visibility (GEO) audits to local businesses, deliver done-for-you research reports built on Sparkpages, outsource appointment-verification calls with the Call For Me agent, or resell pitch decks and market analysis produced in minutes. Free tier gives 100-200 credits a day, so you can test the exact workflow you plan to bill for before paying anything.

4.7 Free daily credits + Plus ~$24/mo
