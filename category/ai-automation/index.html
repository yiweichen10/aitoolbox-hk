Zapier AI

Editor's Choice

The leading automation platform now featuring AI to build workflows, process data, and create persistent AI agents.

4.7 Free (100 tasks/mo) | Starter $19.99/mo | Professional $49/mo | Team $69/mo

Make

A powerful visual automation platform that lets you design, build, and automate anything from simple tasks to complex workflows without coding.

4.8 Free (1,000 ops/mo)

Gumloop

Gumloop is a no-code agentic automation platform for building complex AI workflows. It allows users to chain multiple AI models together with loops, reasoning nodes, and human-in-the-loop approvals.

4.9 Free + Pro $37/mo

Bardeen

An AI-powered browser automation platform that builds workflows from plain English descriptions. Connect 5,000+ apps and automate repetitive tasks without writing code.

4.6 Free (100 ops/mo); Pro $15/mo (annual)

Browser Use

Verified

An open-source framework that lets AI agents control your web browser. You give it a natural language instruction — fill a form, scrape product data, book appointments, submit applications — and it drives the browser to get it done. In 2026, Browser Use added smart element detection and multi-tab parallelism, making it genuinely useful for people who sell browser automation as a service.

4.5 Open-source free, cloud tier from $19/mo

Activepieces

Verified

Open-source no-code automation platform. You build workflows that connect 200+ apps — Slack, GitHub, Google Sheets, Stripe, Notion — without writing code. The self-hostable version means your client data stays on your own server, not on a SaaS platform that might triple its prices next year. If you sell automation services to businesses, Activepieces is how you keep margins high while undercutting Zapier.

4.5 Free (self-hosted) + Cloud $19/mo + Enterprise custom

Relevance AI

Trending

Relevance AI is a visual platform for building AI agents and automated workflows without writing backend code. The business angle is straightforward: build custom AI agents for small and mid-size businesses that have real problems but no engineering team to solve them. A lead qualification agent that reads inbound emails and scores prospects? $2,000 build fee plus $200/month maintenance. A customer onboarding agent that auto-generates setup guides from CRM data? $1,500-$3,000 per client. I have been building on Relevance since mid-2025 and the market for 'I need AI but I do not know how' is bigger than most developers realize.

4.5 Free + Pro from $49/mo

Flowise

Verified

Flowise is an open-source drag-and-drop platform for building AI chatbots, RAG apps, and LLM workflows — no coding required. You wire together components (models, vector stores, document loaders, API endpoints) on a visual canvas, hit deploy, and you have a production-ready API. The money angle: I use it to build custom AI chatbots for local businesses ($1,500-$3,000 setup + $200-$500/mo retainer), and the self-hosted Docker version costs me zero. Three clients covers my rent. You can also sell pre-built workflow templates, offer chatbot-as-a-service, or run AI automation agencies with enterprise clients who want RAG over their internal docs but cannot afford a $50K custom solution.

4.5 Free self-host + Cloud $25/mo

Apify

Trending

Apify is the scraping-and-automation platform a lot of AI data pipelines quietly sit on. You run ready-made Actors to pull web pages, social feeds, search results and maps data, or you write your own Actor and sell it in the Apify Store. The money angle is the real draw: publish an Actor, set a price per event, and keep roughly 80% of every sale while Apify handles billing, hosting and proxies. It is built for people who are fine with APIs, JSON and a bit of JavaScript, not for someone who wants a click-and-go dashboard.

4.6 Free + Starter $19/mo · Scale $199/mo
