Best AI Research Tools 2026

3 tools reviewed and ranked

Elicit

Verified

Elicit is an AI research assistant for academic literature - it searches 138 million papers and 545,000 clinical trials, extracts sample sizes, methods and outcomes into structured tables, and backs every claim with a sentence-level citation you can click through. I run literature reviews as a service on it: a $150-$500 scoping review costs me roughly $0-2 in tool fees on the free-to-Pro plans, and systematic review support for health and med-tech clients bills at $2K-$10K per engagement. It grew out of Ought, the non-profit AI lab, and counts 2M+ researchers among its users; the 2026 version adds Research Agents, PRISMA-style screening and an API.

⭐ 4.6 Free + Pro from $49/user/mo

Scite

Verified

Scite analyzes 1.6B+ citation statements to label every citation as supporting, contrasting or mentioning - the fastest way to check if a claim holds up. Sell evidence dossiers and cite-checking retainers: one review pays for a year of Pro.

⭐ 4.5 From $12/mo · Pro $40/mo annual

Litmaps

Verified

Litmaps turns a seed paper into a visual citation map and alerts you when new work connects to it. Sell literature-review sprints and research monitoring retainers instead of doing manual searches.

⭐ 4.6 Free + Pro $10/mo · $120/yr annual
