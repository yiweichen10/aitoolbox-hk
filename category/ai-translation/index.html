DeepL

Editor's Choice

DeepL is the world's most accurate AI translation tool, renowned for natural-sounding translations powered by deep learning. Ideal for professionals and students.

4.2 Free + Pro $10/mo

Crowdin

Trending

Crowdin is the localization platform small software teams move to once a spreadsheet stops working - upload source strings, translate with AI plus human post-editors, and ship every language back through the API on each release. The free tier is a working product, and the hosted-word model (source words x target languages) makes client jobs easy to price: a 5,000-word app into 10 languages invoices at $4,000-6,000 while tool and translation costs land near $1,500-3,000.

4.5 Free + Pro from $50/mo · Team $150/mo

Lokalise

Trending

Lokalise is the continuous-localization platform product teams pick once they ship to several markets and a spreadsheet stops scaling - connect a repo, push source strings, and ship every language back through the API or over the air. The plan-tier, unlimited-hosted-words model makes client jobs easy to price: a 5,000-word app into 10 languages invoices at $4,000-6,000 while your tool cost stays at $144/month, so the localization-as-a-service margin is the whole game.

4.5 Explorer $144/mo · Growth $375/mo

Phrase

Trending

Phrase is the enterprise localization platform (the old Memsource) that product and language teams reach for when the job is bigger than a spreadsheet and needs governed machine translation. You wire up repos, design files and support tools, then Phrase returns every language through its TMS, the Strings product for software UI, or the Language AI layer. The seat-plus-managed-words billing makes the money angle clear: a 5,000-word app into 10 languages invoices at $4,000-6,000 while your own tool cost on the Freelancer plan is $27/month, so selling localization as a service is where the margin lives.

4.6 From $27/mo · Team $1,245/mo

Smartling

Trending

Smartling is the enterprise localization platform that bundles AI translation with managed translation services and certified project managers - no list prices, contracts median $60K/year per Vendr data. For marketing-heavy teams shipping into 10+ languages it replaces both a TMS and an agency. Freelancers earn by running client Smartling instances as localization-ops consultants.

4.5 Free Core tier · quote-based (~$60K/yr median)
