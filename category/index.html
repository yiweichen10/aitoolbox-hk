Questions about AI tool categories

What categories of AI tools are there?

AI Tool Lab organizes its 291 independently reviewed AI tools into 22 categories: AI Income, AI Video, AI Image, AI Development, AI Writing, AI Audio, AI Coding, AI Design, AI Marketing, AI Chat, AI Productivity, AI E-commerce, AI Automation, AI Agents, AI Search, AI SEO, AI Translation, AI Website Builder, AI Office, Market Research, AI Research, AI API. The most extensive are AI Income (57), AI Video (28), AI Image (22), AI Development (20), AI Writing (17). Every category page ranks its tools by editor score and states the price model of each tool.

I'm new to AI tools — where should I start?

Start with AI Chat: these tools are the most general-purpose and cover Q&A, writing, translation and code explanation with no learning curve. Then move into a vertical category that matches your work: AI Writing for content, AI Image for visuals, AI Coding for programming, and AI Office for reports and presentations.

Are there free AI tools in these categories?

Yes. Almost every category includes both free and paid tools, most commonly a free tier with a paid subscription on top. Each tool page states its price model, and the quick-reference table above links straight to every category so you can compare prices.

What is the fastest way to find the right AI tool?

Three ways: use the quick-reference table on this page to match your task to a category; open a category page and sort by editor score; or read one of our hands-on comparison articles when you have two or three candidates and need a verdict.

How often are the categories updated?

The tool library is updated daily and the category structure grows as new tools are added. This page was last updated on 2026-11-22: 22 categories, 291 reviewed tools.
