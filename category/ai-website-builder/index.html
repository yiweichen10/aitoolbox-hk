Webflow AI

Verified

Webflow is a visual website builder that lets you design and launch sites without writing code. Their AI assistant — built directly into the editor — generates page layouts, writes copy, audits SEO, and suggests design improvements from natural language prompts. If you build websites for money, this is the tool that turns a 40-hour client project into a 15-hour one.

4.6 Free + Core from $29/mo

Durable

Trending

Durable is an AI business builder that generates a full website in about 30 seconds from one prompt, then bolts on CRM, booking, invoicing, and an AI assistant that writes your marketing for you. Over 3 million businesses have been built on it. The money angle is direct: if you sell website or local-business services, you can spin up a client site in an afternoon and bill for days of agency work.

4.3 Free + Launch from $25/mo

10Web

Trending

10Web is an agentic AI website builder that generates a full WordPress site from a prompt, an existing URL or a Figma file, then hosts it on managed Google Cloud. The money is not in the subscription: on the agency plans you get a white-label dashboard you can resell at $50-200/mo per site against a ~$4/site platform cost, and the Redesign agent turns slow, dated client sites into fast productized rebuilds you can finish in a day.

4.1 From $10/mo · Agency $42.5/mo

Elementor

The WordPress page builder behind millions of sites, now bundling AI copy, images and CSS into every tier. The money angle is the classic one: client builds pay for the license many times over, maintenance retainers compound, and template kits sell while you sleep.

3.4 Free core · Pro from $60/yr

Base44

Verified

Wix-owned vibe-coding builder that turns a sentence into a working full-stack app - database, login, and hosting included. The credit math (25 free messages a month, $16/mo for 100) makes it the cheapest way to ship client micro-apps, MVPs, and internal tools without touching code.

3.1 Free · Starter from $16/mo

Carrd

Verified

A one-page site builder at $19 per year - the cheapest legitimate way to put a funnel, link-in-bio or product checkout on your own domain, with signup forms into 20+ email platforms and zero platform fees on payments.

3.3 Free + Pro from $9/yr
