Doba Pilot

Doba Pilot is an AI-powered dropshipping agent that automates product sourcing, listing, and inventory management, focusing on high-quality US-based suppliers.

4.5 $29 - $249/mo

Klaviyo

Trending

Klaviyo is the email and SMS platform most Shopify and DTC brands already run their retention on, which Klaviyo now markets as a B2C CRM with AI built in. The money is not in reselling it — it is in running lifecycle email for stores that have a list and nobody managing it, at $1,500-$5,000/mo per client.

4.5 Free + Email $20/mo · Email+SMS $35/mo

Shopify

Trending

Shopify is the frame most online stores run on — hosting, checkout and an admin a non-developer can run, now with Sidekick and Shopify Magic AI helpers. The money is not in the dashboard screenshots; it is in building stores for clients at $2,000–$10,000 a build, collecting the free Partner referral cut (20% recurring, up to $2,300 for Plus), or shipping an app on the 80% revenue share.

4.5 Starter $5 · Basic $39/mo · Plus $2,300/mo

BigCommerce

Verified

Open SaaS storefront platform built for merchants who outgrow the app-tax model: B2B price lists, customer groups and multi-currency ship natively, and the API layer stays open. The money is in building and migrating stores for clients, not reselling the platform.

4.2 From $29/mo · Scale $299/mo

WooCommerce

Verified

The free open-source storefront that runs on WordPress and answers to nobody: no revenue share, no plan tiers, any payment gateway without penalty. The money is in building and maintaining stores for clients - or selling your own extensions into a huge plugin market.

4.3 Free core · Hosting from $25/mo

Helium 10

Verified

The all-in-one suite for Amazon (and now Walmart and TikTok Shop) sellers: product research, keyword data, listing AI, PPC automation and profit tracking. The money angle is the most direct on this site - the tool exists to find products that sell, and people charge real money for doing exactly that.

4.5 Free plan · Paid from $29/mo
