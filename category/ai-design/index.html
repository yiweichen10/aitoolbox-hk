Canva AI

Canva AI is a powerful AI design tool with smart features like AI image generation, background removal, and automated layouts for creating professional designs effortlessly.

4.5 Free + Pro from $14.99/mo

Brandmark

An AI-powered logo and brand identity generator that creates unique logos, color palettes, and font pairings with a one-time payment model.

4.5 Basic $25 (one-time)

Beautiful.ai

Beautiful.ai is a rules-based presentation tool that uses AI to automatically design slides as you add content. It ensures professional layouts and brand consistency without manual formatting.

4.7 Pro $12/mo | Team $40/mo

Napkin AI

Napkin AI is a visual storyteller that converts text into professional diagrams, flowcharts, and infographics instantly. It helps business professionals explain complex ideas visually without design skills.

4.7 Free | Plus $10/mo | Pro $25/mo

Relume AI

Trending

Relume AI is the ultimate site-building ally for Webflow and Figma. It generates AI-powered sitemaps and wireframes in minutes, using a library of over 1,000 professional components to speed up your design workflow.

4.9 Free | Starter $18/mo | Pro $40/mo

AdCreative AI

Verified

AdCreative AI generates high-converting ad creatives and social media posts using artificial intelligence. It's built for marketers who need to scale their ad production and improve ROI without hiring a full design team.

4.6 Free tier available; Paid plans $29-$299/mo

Framer AI

Trending

AI-powered web design platform that lets you build production websites visually, then export clean React code. The monetization angle is direct: instead of spending weeks coding a site from scratch, you design in Framer with AI generating layouts, copy, and images as you go, and either publish directly (hosted) or export the code. Freelancers use it to deliver client sites in days instead of weeks. Designers use it to skip the handoff-to-developer bottleneck. The AI fills in placeholder content, suggests page structures, and even generates images inline. You still need design taste — Framer does not make you a designer — but it removes the technical friction between idea and published website.

4.6 Free + Pro $20/mo + Team $50/mo (billed yearly: $15/mo, $40/mo)

Figma AI

Trending

The AI layer baked into the world's most-used UI design tool. Figma's AI generates layouts from text prompts, auto-renames layers, suggests components, and produces design-to-code specs. For people who sell design services, the real value is speed — what used to take 6 hours of wireframing now takes 90 minutes of AI-assisted layout plus polish. You serve more clients per month on the same hours.

4.2 Free (limited) + Pro $15/mo + Org $45/mo

Spline AI

Verified

Spline AI lets you create 3D models, scenes, and interactive prototypes by just describing what you want in plain English — no 3D software skills required. Upload a sketch, write a text prompt, and it generates a usable 3D asset in seconds. The money angle: freelancers and small studios use it to offer 3D web experiences, AR filters, and game-ready assets to clients who'd normally pay $500-$2,000 per model. You do it in an afternoon instead of a week.

4.5 Free tier + Pro $12/mo

Relume

Verified

Relume turns your sitemap into a full wireframe and Figma-ready component library in about 30 seconds. I have been using it for client website projects since late 2024, and the honest value proposition is speed. A landing page that used to take me 6-10 hours of wireframing now takes 2-3 hours from sitemap to polished Figma file. At $75-$125/hour for freelance web design, that is $450-$875 in saved time per project — or charged as additional margin if you are billing flat-rate. The sitemap-to-wireframe AI is the standout feature: paste a URL or type a page description, it spits out a structured layout with nav, hero, features, testimonials, footer — all editable. Their component library (1,000+ pre-built sections) syncs directly into Figma as native layers, not flattened images. Free tier is 3 projects, Pro plan at $26/month handles around 15-20 projects/month. The AI output still needs a human pass — maybe 30% of auto-generated layouts need manual rearranging — but starting from an 80%-complete wireframe beats a blank canvas any day.

4.6 Free (3 projects) + Starter $15/mo + Pro $26/mo + Agency custom

Looka

Verified

AI logo and brand-kit builder that turns a business name and a few style picks into dozens of logo concepts plus a full brand package - business cards, social sets, and a brand guide. A fast, cheap way for freelancers to deliver client-ready branding and keep most of the fee.

4.5 From $20 (pay per download)

Wix AI

Verified

Wix AI is the AI layer baked into Wix, the all-in-one website builder - specifically the 2026 Wix Harmony editor and its built-in agent Aria. You describe your business in plain English - 'plumbing company in Austin with a booking page and a services list' - and Harmony generates a complete multi-page site with real copy, images and layout in about three minutes. Then Aria keeps working with you: 'make the header more modern', 'add a testimonial section', 'write a blog post' - it edits the live site while you watch. No code, no design skills, no blank canvas. I build websites for small local businesses for a living, and the money angle is straightforward: a plumber or dentist who would pay a design agency $3K and wait six weeks for a site gets a solid one from me in a day for a fraction of that, and I hand over the keys so they own the thing. Wix is my delivery vehicle - the AI does the first draft, I do the polish, and the client pays me for judgment and speed, not for dragging pixels around.

4.8 Free + Light from $17/mo
