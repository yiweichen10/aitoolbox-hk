Freepik AI

Editor's Choice

Freepik AI design suite built into the world's biggest stock asset library. Generates images, removes backgrounds, upscales photos, and creates mockups in a few clicks. Editor's Choice

Real-time AI generation platform where images and videos render as you draw or type. Photoroom

Editor's Choice

AI photo editing platform built specifically for e-commerce product photography. The monetization angle is straightforward: instead of paying a photographer $200/session for product shots, you pay $9.99/month and generate studio-quality product images in minutes. Practical for Amazon FBA sellers, Etsy shop owners, print-on-demand merchants, and dropshippers who need consistent product visuals at scale without hiring a photographer or renting a studio. Also useful for real estate agents creating virtual staging, and social media sellers who need daily product posts.

Flux

Topaz Photo AI

Verified

Desktop AI photo enhancer from Topaz Labs that removes noise, sharpens soft focus, and upscales small images without turning them to mush. The cleanup engine behind a real photo-restoration and enhancement side business - it turns denoise, sharpen, and upscale from evening-long grinds into billable minutes.

⭐ 4.7 From $199 one-time

Magnific AI

Verified

Magnific AI is the generative image upscaler that went from indie tool to the finishing step for AI art: it takes a soft, low-res image - a 512px Midjourney render, an SD sketch, a phone photo - and upscales it up to 16x while inventing plausible new detail (pores, fabric weave, foliage, reflections) instead of just stretching pixels. It also does style transfer, relight and inpainting, and it runs in the browser on Freepik's GPU farm, so no expensive hardware on your end. I make and sell AI art for a living, and the money angle is that Magnific is the difference between a render that looks 'AI-generated' and a print-ready image you can sell: prints, POD listings, game concept art for clients, digital commissions - the upscale is the step where the work stops looking like a draft. It is not cheap (from $39/mo, no free tier, tokens expire monthly) and it will happily hallucinate detail into faces and logos it shouldn't touch, so it is a tool for people who control the output, not a magic button.

⭐ 4.6 From $39/mo (no free tier)

Clipdrop

Trending

Clipdrop is an AI image-editing toolkit that bundles the jobs most people open Photoshop for - background removal, 16x upscaling, object cleanup, relight, and uncrop - into one browser tab. I run a done-for-you product-photo service for e-commerce sellers, and Clipdrop is the bench I use for 90% of it. The money side is blunt: a seller hands me 40 raw phone photos, I return 40 clean, white-background, marketplace-ready images the same afternoon, and bill $300-$800 for the batch while the software costs $9. The skill you sell is judgment - which shot is worth relighting, where the edge is ragged - not the clicking, which Clipdrop does in seconds.

⭐ 4.5 Free $0 + Pro $9/mo (some listings show $15; API from ~$49/mo via Jasper)

Let's Enhance

Verified

Let's Enhance is a browser-based AI tool that upscales and cleans images. It takes a blurry, low-resolution photo and turns it into a sharp 4K or 8K file, fixes JPEG artifacts, removes noise, and restores old damaged pictures. I run a small photo-editing service on the side, and this is the tool I reach for when a client sends a 300-pixel product shot or a crumbling family photo from 1972. The money side is direct: you take work that used to need Photoshop hours and a steady hand, and you hand it back in minutes. The free tier gives 10 one-time credits; paid plans run $9 to $34 a month and upscaling goes up to 16x with print-ready 300 DPI output. It will not replace a retoucher for fine art, but for e-commerce catalogs, real-estate listings, and old-photo restoration jobs, it pays for itself on the first client.

⭐ 4.6 10 free credits + Starter $9/mo

Krea 2

Trending

Krea 2 is Krea's first image model built entirely in-house - not another Flux or Stable Diffusion wrapper. Released June 2026 with open weights, it is built for people who care about how the picture looks, not just whether it appears. The three variants do different jobs: Turbo for fast iteration at roughly three seconds a shot, Medium for stable illustration and anime, Large for the richest photorealism. The real draw is creative control - you can feed it a single reference image or a whole moodboard and it picks up the style, and 'creativity' is an actual slider you turn up when you want the model to interpret instead of copy. The money side is concrete: at $0.015-$0.07 per API image, you can run a print-on-demand shop, a custom-portrait service, or batch product visuals for e-commerce without touching a subscription. The free consumer tier hands out 100 compute units a day, so you can prototype every billable workflow before spending a dollar.

⭐ 4.6 Free + Basic from $9/mo
