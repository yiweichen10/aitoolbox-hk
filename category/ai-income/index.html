Whop

Verified

Whop is a storefront where creators sell communities, courses, software access, and digital downloads, then get paid without wiring together five separate tools. If your plan is to charge for access - a Discord group, a template pack, a mini-course - it handles checkout, delivery, and Discord gating in one place.

4.3 Free + 3% fee (direct) / 30% (marketplace)

Gumroad

Verified

Gumroad is a checkout and delivery service for digital products - ebooks, templates, prompts, software licenses, memberships. You upload a file or paste a link, set a price, and get a URL that takes payments the same day. If your goal is to turn something you made into income without building a store, it handles payment, file delivery, and global tax collection so you only deal with the selling.

4.2 Free + 10% per sale (or 30% via Discover)

Patreon

Verified

Patreon is a membership billing system for creators - you set up tiers ($5 for bonus posts, $15 for a private Discord and monthly assets), and fans subscribe and get charged every month on autopilot. Patreon takes its cut, processes the cards, handles tax for foreign fans, and pays you the rest. If your goal is turning an audience you already have into steady recurring income, it does the plumbing so you focus on the content behind the tiers.

4.1 Free + 10% platform fee (legacy plans 5-12%)

Substack

Verified

Substack is a newsletter platform with billing built in - you write posts, they go out by email and live on your page, and you can put a paywall in front of the paid stuff. Readers subscribe monthly or yearly, Substack collects the money and handles delivery, and you keep what's left after its cut. For a writer who wants to turn a topic into recurring income, it is the lowest-risk place to start: publishing is free, and you only pay once a reader actually pays you.

4.4 Free to publish + 10% of paid revenue (plus Stripe fees)

Ko-fi

Verified

Ko-fi is a creator monetisation hub where your audience sends one-off tips ("coffees"), monthly memberships, buys from your shop, or books commissions - all from one free page, with money paid straight into your own PayPal or Stripe. The pull for making money is the 0% fee on tips: you keep every coffee after just card processing, so a blog, YouTube channel, or podcast can start collecting support the same day with no monthly cost. Add a $3-5/month membership or a small digital shop and it becomes a real income line without building a store.

4.6 Free to start - 0% on tips, 5% on shop/memberships (0% with Ko-fi Gold $6/mo)

Skool

Verified

Skool is a community platform where you charge people a monthly, annual, or one-time fee to join your group and take your courses - all in one app with a feed, a classroom, a calendar, and points that keep members coming back. The money draw is simple: on its Pro plan Skool takes 0% of what you charge. You pay a flat $99/month and keep everything else after Stripe's ~2.9% + $0.30 per payment. If you already have an audience that pays for coaching, a mastermind, or a course, Skool lets you run the whole thing from a phone and stop bleeding 8-12% to Patreon or up to 30% to Whop.

4.7 Pro $99/mo flat - 0% Skool fee (Stripe 2.9% + $0.30); 14-day free trial, no card

Stan Store

Verified

Stan Store turns your Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube bio link into a one-page mobile store. Instead of sending followers to a Linktree of outbound links, you put a real checkout there: sell PDFs, templates, mini-courses, coaching calls, and memberships, and buyers pay without leaving the app. The money case is the hook - Stan charges a flat $29 or $99 a month and takes 0% of your sales. You keep everything after Stripe's ~2.9% plus $0.30. If you already have even a few thousand warm followers, that is the cheapest way to start selling your own digital products without building a website.

4.7 From $29/mo (14-day trial)

Kajabi

Verified

Kajabi bundles course hosting, email marketing, sales funnels, a website builder, and community tools into one dashboard, so a creator can run an entire paid knowledge business without stitching together five subscriptions. If your plan is to sell a course, a membership, or coaching calls and keep the margin, it replaces the stack.

4.4 From ~$179/mo (Basic) / $249 Growth / $499 Pro

Lemon Squeezy

Verified

Lemon Squeezy is a merchant of record for digital products and software subscriptions. It takes the payment, calculates and remits VAT and sales tax in every country, delivers license keys, and runs an affiliate program. A solo maker can sell worldwide without setting up a company in each region or hiring a tax service.

4.3 5% + 50¢ per sale (no monthly fee) / Sweet $29/mo

Polar

Verified

Polar is a merchant of record and billing stack built for developers selling software, SaaS, and AI products. It acts as the legal seller on every receipt, so it collects and remits VAT, GST, and sales tax across 100+ markets while you keep building. For an indie maker shipping a token-metered API or a small SaaS, it turns a checkout link into a global storefront without a tax lawyer.

4.5 Free Starter 5% + 50¢/sale / Pro $20 / Growth $100 / Scale $400 per month

Fourthwall

Verified

Fourthwall is an all-in-one storefront for creators who already have an audience on YouTube, Twitch, or TikTok and want to turn that reach into recurring income. It runs print-on-demand merch, paid memberships, digital downloads, and tips from one branded site you own, with no monthly fee until you sell. For a streamer or video creator it replaces Patreon plus a merch vendor plus a tip jar with a single checkout that carries your name, not a marketplace's.

4.4 Free $0/mo (5% memberships, 0% self-shipped merch) / Pro $19 per month

Payhip

Verified

Payhip is a no-monthly-fee storefront for selling digital downloads, courses, memberships, and coaching. You keep 100% of revenue on the Pro plan and Payhip files EU and UK VAT for you, so a solo creator can open a global shop without a tax setup.

4.5 Free 5% / Plus $29 / Pro $99 per mo

Paddle

Verified

Paddle is a merchant of record for software and SaaS: it acts as the legal seller, files VAT and sales tax in 200+ countries, and takes 5% + $0.50 per sale with no monthly fee. Ship a paid app or AI tool globally without a tax team.

4.3 5% + $0.50 per sale, no monthly fee

Printify

Trending

Print-on-demand platform that turns your designs into 1,300+ physical products and ships them after each sale. No inventory, no upfront cost, no fulfilment headaches - you keep the margin.

4.6 Free + Premium $24.99/mo

Kit

Trending

Email marketing built for creators, formerly ConvertKit. Grow a list for free up to 10,000 subscribers, then make money from it with built-in digital product sales, tip jars, and paid newsletter subscriptions.

4.7 Free up to 10k subs, from $39/mo

Beacons.ai

Verified

Link-in-bio page and creator storefront in one: sell digital products, run a media kit, collect tips, and pitch brands from a single free page. The cheapest way to turn a social following into income.

4.5 Free + plans from $10/mo

Etsy

Verified

The marketplace where you can sell digital products - templates, printables, wall art - to 80M+ existing buyers without building an audience first. The money angle: list a $9 planner template once and Etsy's search traffic sells it while you sleep. Fees take 10-25% per sale, but you skip months of audience building.

4.3 $0.20 to list + ~10% per sale

Fiverr

Verified

The biggest fixed-price freelance marketplace where you sell services - AI automation builds, video editing, writing, design - to buyers who already have a budget. The money angle: package an AI-assisted skill into a gig, let Fiverr's search traffic bring clients, and grow from a $50 order into monthly retainers. Fiverr keeps 20% and new sellers start slow, but entry costs nothing.

4.4 Free to sell · 20% per sale

PromptBase

Verified

The marketplace where prompt engineers sell tested prompts for ChatGPT, Claude, Midjourney and more - free to list, 80% revenue share on marketplace sales and 0% when buyers come through your own link.

4.2 Free to list · keep 80%

Wirestock

Verified

Wirestock was the one-upload-to-every-stock-site shortcut; since January 2026 it no longer sends new work to Adobe Stock or Shutterstock, and instead pays creators through AI training-data licensing and paid creative projects. If you have a pile of photos, illustrations or AI art, this review covers what the pivot actually means, what still earns, and whether the new model is worth your upload time.

4.0 Free to join · commission-based

Amazon KDP

Verified

Amazon's free self-publishing platform - publish ebooks and print-on-demand paperbacks with up to 70% royalties, Kindle Unlimited page-read income, and Amazon's book-buying traffic already in the door

4.3 Free to publish · up to 70% royalty

Udemy

Verified

The world's biggest online course marketplace: publish free, teach 70M+ students, and earn 97% on students you bring yourself - 37% on the ones Udemy sends you.

4.5 Free to teach · 37–97% revenue share

Upwork

Verified

The largest bidding-style freelance marketplace and the best-paying venue for AI-adjacent skills: automation builds, chatbot setup, and AI content ops bill $60-150/hr, with escrow-protected contracts that run for months instead of one-off gigs.

4.2 Free to join · ~10% per contract

Redbubble

Verified

Free print-on-demand marketplace with built-in buyer traffic: upload a design once, sell it on 60+ products, and let Redbubble handle printing and shipping. The zero-audience way to test AI art for money - just know new sellers keep only half their margin until they reach a better tier.

3.8 Free to sell · 50% fee to start

Skillshare

Verified

Subscription course marketplace where teachers get paid from a shared fund based on paid-member watch time. Free to teach, built-in creative audience - the practical way to earn from teaching AI skills without a storefront.

3.9 Free to teach · paid by watch time

Buy Me a Coffee

Verified

One-page creator monetization: one-time tips, monthly memberships, and digital extras with zero monthly fees and a flat 5% cut. The fastest way to find out if your AI content actually earns - live in five minutes.

4.5 Free · 5% per transaction

Adobe Stock

Verified

Adobe Stock's contributor program is the biggest officially AI-friendly stock marketplace: upload photos, vectors, video or AI-generated images, tick one label box, and earn 33-35% royalties from Creative Cloud's enterprise buyer base. Slow to start (45-day payout lock, cents per download) but the files compound for years and nothing is exclusive.

4.5 Free to join · 33-35% royalty

YouTube

Verified

YouTube's Partner Program is the biggest creator monetization pool on the internet: 55% of long-form ad revenue, 70% of fan funding, and Premium payouts stacked on top. For AI-tool users it is the natural endpoint - every script, voice, and editing tool on this site feeds a channel, and long-form RPMs of $5-$15 in finance and tech beat every other platform per view. The catch is the 2025 inauthentic-content policy: the money is real, but the AI-slop lane is closed. This page breaks down the tiers, the honest Shorts math, and what 'human creative input' actually means before you upload.

4.6 Free · 55% long-form, 45% Shorts

Amazon Associates

Verified

The world's largest affiliate program, running since 1996. Pair it with AI-generated buying guides, review posts, and YouTube videos to earn 1-10% commission on Amazon sales your content drives - the lowest-friction way to turn content into commission checks, if you accept the small print.

4.4 Free to join · 1–10% per sale

Outlier

Verified

Outlier (by Scale AI) pays you to train AI models - ranking responses, debugging code, and grading reasoning in your field of expertise. No audience, no product, no clients: the only tool in this category that turns existing knowledge into weekly cash, $15-50+/hr.

4.0 Free to join · $15-50/hr

Medium

Verified

Medium pays writers a cut of its membership pool whenever paying members read their stories - no clients, no products, no hosting bill, and the platform brings built-in readers. It is the lowest-friction paid publishing setup that exists, and also the easiest place to publish for months and earn almost nothing if you skip the mechanics.

4.0 Free to write · paid by member reads

UserTesting

Verified

UserTesting pays you $10 to use a website or app for 20 minutes while thinking out loud on camera. No product, no audience, no clients - the fastest route to a first dollar in this category, if you can stomach the screener rejection rate.

4.1 Free · $10 per 20-min test

User Interviews

Verified

User Interviews pays $50-150 for a 30-60 minute research interview and $100-250 for focus groups - the highest effective hourly rate in the get-paid-for-your-opinion category, if your profile survives the 5-15% selection funnel.

4.5 Free to join · $50-$150 per interview

Twitch

Verified

Twitch is the largest live-streaming platform, and its monetization stack - subs, Bits, ads, Plus Program - turns a small, loyal live audience into recurring income. For AI-tool users, it is the highest-trust format: a real person on camera or on mic, with AI tools handling moderation, overlays, clip cutting and multi-platform distribution in the background.

4.5 Free · 50/50 subs, up to 70/30

DistroKid

Verified

DistroKid is the pipe between your music files and Spotify, Apple Music, TikTok and 150+ stores - pay one flat annual fee, upload unlimited songs, keep 100% of what the streams pay. For anyone making music with AI tools, it is the cheapest working route from a Suno export to actual streaming royalties, as long as you understand the disclosure rules and the 1,000-stream floor before you upload.

4.5 From $24.99/yr · keep 100%

TikTok

Verified

TikTok is the fastest discovery engine on the internet and, through its Creator Rewards Program, a way to turn original 60-second-plus videos into view-based income. The catch: only qualified views pay, the RPM is a fraction of YouTube's, and the real money for most creators comes from brand deals and TikTok Shop - which makes it a funnel, not a paycheck.

4.3 Free · $0.40-$1.00 per 1k views

Amazon Merch on Demand

Verified

Amazon's invite-only print-on-demand program: upload designs, Amazon prints, ships via Prime and handles returns. Zero upfront cost with Amazon buyer traffic built in - but the June 2026 royalty restructure halved base payouts, so the real money sits in external-traffic tiers and higher-ticket products.

4.0 Free · ~$2.44-$5.27 per $19.99 tee

Spotify for Creators

Verified

Spotify for Creators is free unlimited podcast hosting plus the Partner Program that splits ad revenue 50/50 and pays Premium video revenue - the gate dropped to 2,000 hours and 1,000 listeners in January 2026, which makes it the first real paycheck most small audio shows can reach. For AI creators it is the distribution half of the AI podcast pipeline: NotebookLM or ElevenLabs drafts the audio, Spotify delivers it to 600M+ users and pays - as long as no AI voice is impersonating anyone and you insert ad breaks, because without them you earn zero.

4.5 Free hosting · 50% ad share, 10% on subs

Contra

Verified

Contra is the freelance platform that takes 0% of what you earn - no commission on services, no cut of digital products, with contracts and escrow built in. The catch lives on the client's side: a flat $15/$29 payment fee on the free plan that Pro ($29/mo) removes. For AI service sellers it is the cheapest place to bill automation builds, chatbot setups, and content operations - and the natural landing spot for clients you met on Fiverr or Upwork, so a marketplace stops taking 20% of every invoice.

4.3 Free · 0% commission

Google AdSense

Verified

Google's publisher ad network: paste one snippet, its auction fills your pages, and you keep about 68% of what advertisers pay on Google Ads demand, accrued per impression. The lowest-effort way to make raw AI-content traffic pay without selling anything - and the lowest-paying tier of the ad ladder, on an account Google can close near-permanently over traffic you never faked.

3.8 Free to join · keep ~68%

ClickBank

Verified

The veteran affiliate marketplace for digital products: free to join, 60-day cookies, public conversion stats, and 50-75% commissions that make one sale worth ten Amazon sales. Payout rules punish the inattentive - plan around the five-sale gate.

3.9 Free to join · 50–75% per sale

Creative Market

Verified

Curated marketplace to sell fonts, graphics, templates and mockups to professional designers. Application-gated shop, 50% default split, real buyer traffic with no ad spend - a clean passive-income lane for AI-assisted asset packs that survive human curation.

3.8 Free to sell · 50% default split

Canva Creators

Verified

Submit templates and elements into Canva's own library and earn royalties from a monthly pool whenever 200M+ users use your work. Free to apply, no follower gate - but the payout rate is a black box and AI-generated submissions are banned.

3.8 Free · usage-based royalty pool

Flippa

Verified

Open marketplace to sell websites, AI tools, micro-SaaS, apps, newsletters and domains. Largest buyer pool online, no revenue gate, listing packages from $29 - the exit ramp for assets you built and monetized with AI.

3.9 List from $29 · 10% success fee

PartnerStack

Verified

Affiliate network built only for B2B software: 600+ SaaS brands pay recurring commissions - typically 20-40% - every month your referral keeps paying. Free to join, $5 payout floor, 90-day cookies. The recurring-income piece Amazon and ClickBank leave empty.

4.5 Free to join · 20-40% recurring

Notion Marketplace

Verified

Notion's official template store inside an app with 100M+ users: you publish a workspace template, Notion handles checkout, tax, delivery and takes 8% plus 40 cents per sale. The trust badge and built-in audience are why sellers keep a listing here even though Gumroad charges less - and since March 2026 you can also sell Custom Agents, not just templates.

4.5 Free to list · 8% + $0.40 per sale

X Creator Rewards

Verified

X's creator payout program rebuilt from scratch in September 2026: the old ads revenue sharing died on September 7, and Original Content Rewards now pays on qualified impressions from Premium users' home timelines - original content only. The entry bar dropped tenfold to 500K Premium timeline views per 90 days, but '500 followers' now means 500 verified followers, and the published rates are the lowest of any major platform. For AI-assisted writers it is the cheapest content production in the game and the weakest direct payout in it.

4.0 Premium from $8/mo · paid per qualified view
