Google Trends tells you a topic scores 73 out of 100. It never tells you that 73 means 190,000 searches a month, or whether the slope next quarter is up and to the right or a cliff. Glimpse is the browser extension that fixes exactly that: same Google Trends interface you know, overlaid with absolute search volumes back to 2004, growth rates, 12-month forecasts, seasonality panels and alerts. Because it rides on Google's own data rather than a keyword-tool panel, the numbers carry different weight than modeled estimates from third-party SEO suites.

Pricing, and the missing pricing page

There is no public pricing page - plans appear only inside the product after signup. Free covers 10 lookups a month with full data on each. Pro runs $99/mo for 100 lookups, 25 tracked keywords, about 24 alerts, 10 exports and discovery capped at 12 rising trends per category. Expert is $299/mo: 500 lookups, unlimited alerts, bulk tracking and the full 1M+ trend database. Enterprise adds API access, custom dashboards and invoicing by quote. Annual billing gives two months free. The free plan and the $99 entry are confirmed on Glimpse's own guides; the tier structure comes from an August 2026 capture of the in-app upgrade screen, so verify limits inside your own account before budgeting.

How people make money with it

Niche-site builders use the forecast and growth-rate panels to publish into topics months before they peak - the difference between ranking a page into a rising wave versus a dead pond. E-commerce sellers read seasonality panels to time product launches and ad calendars, and the channel breakdown shows whether a rising topic is a TikTok thing or a Reddit thing, which decides where the content budget goes. Agencies sell it directly: a quarterly trend report built from Glimpse exports is a one-hour deliverable that justifies a retainer line, and at $99/mo the subscription pays for itself with one client. Content operators time evergreen refreshes to seasonal spikes - the homepage's own examples (pickleball, mouth tape) were flagged years before they peaked. For the broader research stack, pair it with community listening via GummySearch and audience data from SparkToro, and see our keyword research comparison for where trend data beats volume data.

The honest trade-offs

Ten free lookups is a taste, not a tool - one research session spends them. The Pro tier's discovery cap (12 trends per category) is the upsell hinge; the advertised 1M+ trend database exists only at $299. No API below Enterprise means programmatic monitoring is a sales conversation. Browser support covers Chrome and its Chromium relatives only. And the deepest limitation is conceptual: search interest measures curiosity, not wallets. A topic can trend and never convert - cross-check demand with community pain points and purchase signals before betting a quarter of budget on a rising line.

Questions we get asked

Is Glimpse free to use?

Partly. The free plan gives 10 lookups per month with full data on each - enough to research one topic thoroughly, not enough for ongoing work. Paid access starts at $99/mo (Pro, 100 lookups) and $299/mo (Expert, 500 lookups plus the full trend database), with annual billing giving two months free.

Glimpse or Exploding Topics - which do I need?

They answer different questions. Exploding Topics curates a trend database for you and shows what is rising across categories; Glimpse researches any keyword you type with absolute volume and forecasts on Google's own data. Curated discovery on a budget points to Exploding Topics; research depth on topics you already suspect points to Glimpse. Both appear in our market research tools comparison.

Why is there no public pricing page?

Glimpse gates its plans behind signup - the upgrade screen only appears inside the product after you create an account. A third-party capture of that screen in August 2026 documented the tiers above; the free plan and the $99 starting point are confirmed by Glimpse's own guides, but treat the exact tier limits as subject to change.
