What Sell The Trend Actually Is

Sell The Trend bundles the dropshipping research stack into one subscription: a product-discovery engine (NEXUS), ad spying for Facebook and TikTok, competitor store analysis, supplier connection, one-click product import, and automated order fulfillment. The NEXUS engine watches order momentum across TikTok, Facebook, Amazon and Shopify, then scores each product with the numbers that actually decide whether to test it - sourcing cost, suggested price, profit per sale, break-even CPA, and a saturation meter showing how many stores are already selling it. The company's own public reports pages show the current shape of the data: products at $1-11 sourcing cost selling at $30-50 with 70-90% margins and low single-digit saturation.

It is the middle path between a bare Shopify store with no research tooling and enterprise e-commerce suites. For the surrounding toolset, see our e-commerce AI tools comparison.

The Money Angle

The research stage is where most stores die - this compresses it. The dropshipping funnel is: pick a product, build a store page, buy ads, analyze, iterate. Beginners lose first at picking (saturated products, no margin) and second at ad creative. NEXUS attacks the first failure directly with saturation and margin data, and the Facebook/TikTok ad finders attack the second by showing what proven sellers run. Neither guarantees profit; both cut the cost of being wrong, which is what a research tool is for.

The realistic test budget. A month of Essential ($49.97) plus $200-300 of ad spend is the honest cost of finding out whether this business suits you. The tool's break-even CPA calculation tells you the ceiling you can pay per customer before a product loses money - respect that number and most bad products reject themselves in testing instead of in month three.

Angle for existing sellers. If you already sell on Amazon or Etsy - say through Helium 10 for Amazon keyword work or Printify for POD - Store Intelligence doubles as competitive research: seeing what sells in adjacent stores is useful even if you never dropship. And affiliate sellers running Amazon Associates sites can use TikTok Shop and viral-ad data to spot products before the content wave, then build pages around them.

The Honest Cons

You are paying a fixed subscription for a variable-outcome business - the $400-800 a year due regardless of sales is the first line in your loss column, and no research score changes that. Second, the data is estimation: saturation meters and projected revenue are models of platform signals, not guarantees, and by the time a product surfaces with "low saturation," other subscribers are seeing it too - speed to market is still the differentiator, and the tool does not provide speed. Third, the AI credits are marketing numbers more than capability: 6-20 credits a month covers a store build and a couple of video-ad drafts, then you are back to doing the work. Finally, the tool cannot fix dropshipping's real liabilities - 5-12 day shipping windows, refund rates, ad-account bans - and subscribers who blame the dashboard for those will churn regardless of plan.

Bottom Line

Sell The Trend is a sound buy at exactly one moment: when you are committed to testing dropshipping this quarter and will actually use the research, the ad finders and the fulfillment loop together - Essential on annual billing is the right tier and Lite is not a working plan. It is a poor buy for the merely curious, and a poor substitute for the willingness to spend money on ads and read the numbers. The tool compresses research from hours to minutes; the business still starts where the research ends.

Questions we get asked

Can you actually make money using Sell The Trend?

The tool finds and scores candidates - the money still comes from ads, margins and operations. What it genuinely improves: you start from products with visible demand instead of blind guesses, you see cost-versus-price math (many listings show 70-90% gross margins on sub-$10 sourcing costs), and the ad finders let you model creatives that already worked. Treat every metric as an estimate, budget $200-500 for ad testing beyond the subscription, and treat the tool as the research stage of a business, not the business itself.

What is NEXUS?

NEXUS is Sell The Trend's AI engine that aggregates demand signals across TikTok, Facebook, Amazon and Shopify - order volumes, momentum, saturation, supplier data - into a scored product page with sourcing cost, suggested retail price, profit per sale and break-even CPA. On Lite it runs 10 searches a day; Essential and above are unlimited.

Is the 14-day trial really free?

Yes, but a payment method is required at signup (the company states this is to prevent spam accounts), and the trial converts to a paid subscription automatically unless you cancel before day 14. Calendar the cancellation date if you are only evaluating. There are no contracts and cancellation is one click.
