Metricool is a social media planner and analytics dashboard that covers 10+ networks, competitor tracking and Facebook/Google/TikTok ad accounts in one calendar view. Where Buffer is a straightforward scheduler and Brand24 is listening, Metricool is the portfolio tool freelance social managers run client retainers on - its per-brand pricing mirrors how those retainers are actually billed.

What the plans actually cost

Free: $0 forever - 1 brand, 20 scheduled posts/mo, 5 competitor profiles, 30-day analytics history, AI social assistant, MCP access. Starter: $20/mo for 5 brands or $36/mo for 10 - unlimited publishing under a Fair Use Policy, up to 100 competitor profiles, LinkedIn, PDF/PPT reports, link-in-bio pages, unlimited analytics history. Custom: white label, dedicated manager, custom AI credits. X/Twitter access is a paid add-on at an unpublished price. Prices exclude VAT.

Where the money angle sits

The brand math is the pitch: 10 client brands on Starter costs $36/mo - less than one retainer hour. Freelancers pitch competitor-analysis reports (100 profiles tracked) as the reason their retainer beats a scheduler-only rival. Advanced's approval workflow and Looker Studio connector let a two-person shop run 25 brands without drowning in month-end spreadsheets; white label on Custom turns the whole dashboard into your agency's product. Ad reporting in the same view means the Facebook Ads line item gets reported beside organic reach - clients see one report and you look twice as organized. Creators pair it with vidIQ on YouTube and Predis AI for content generation. Context: our social listening comparison shows where listening specialists still beat built-in monitoring.

The honest drawbacks

X/Twitter behind an add-on with an unpublished price is the kind of thing that surprises you at checkout. The Fair Use Policy caps what 'unlimited' means - bulk-publishing hundreds of posts per brand should read it first. Approval workflows are serviceable but thinner than Sprout-class platforms; teams above roughly 10 people will feel the ceiling. And the interface crams calendar, analytics, ads and competitor data into one dense app - new social managers need a week before it feels fast. Instagram grid previews and some network-specific features trail the native tools.

Bottom line

Metricool is the practical pick for client-based social work: the free plan covers one brand forever, and $36/mo covers ten. Test the free tier with your own brand for a month, then move to Starter before any client signs. Current plans sit on the Metricool pricing page.

Questions we get asked

Is Metricool really free?

Yes - one brand, 20 scheduled posts per month and 30 days of analytics history, forever, with no card. That covers a single own-brand project. Client work needs Starter at $20/mo for 5 brands or $36/mo for 10.

How does Metricool compare to Buffer or Hootsuite?

Buffer is a cleaner pure scheduler; Metricool adds competitor tracking, ad reporting and deeper analytics at a similar price point. Hootsuite costs multiples for fewer brands. Our social media management comparison breaks down the full field.

Can I manage client accounts from one login?

Yes - brands are the unit of management. Starter handles 5-10 brands; Advanced adds team and client management, roles and a post approval system for 15-50 brands. White labeling of the whole dashboard is available on the Custom plan.
