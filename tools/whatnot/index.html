Whatnot is a live-stream auction house for things people collect: trading cards, sneakers, coins, vintage toys, comics and roughly sixty other categories. Sellers run scheduled live shows, buyers bid in real time, and the format changes the economics - auction energy regularly pushes final prices above what the same item fetches at a fixed listing on eBay or Etsy.

The fee structure is the other draw. Commission starts at 8%, and unlike most marketplaces, it is charged on the item price only - shipping and sales tax are excluded from the fee base.

Money Angle

  • Sell where price discovery favors the seller. Live bidding on graded cards, sealed boxes and limited sneakers routinely clears above fixed-price comps. The same item can fetch meaningfully more in a show than in a static listing - that spread is what pays for the fee.
  • Niche authority compounds. Buyers follow sellers, not just categories. A consistent weekly show in one category (vintage watches, Pokémon singles, sports cards) builds a returning audience that marketplace search never will.
  • Use AI where it actually helps: the back office. AI tools earn their keep on comp research across sold-listing history, writing listing titles and show schedules, and drafting post-show invoices - not on the selling itself, which is live, personal and verbal.
  • Both formats count. Live shows and marketplace buy-now sales both count toward your 4-week tier total, so list buy-now inventory between shows to keep volume climbing toward the 7.75% bracket.
  • Start small and ship fast. Apply through the seller application, pass category onboarding, and ship immediately - Whatnot shows dispatch speed on your profile and ranks slow shippers down. Sellers running video-first funnels usually pair this with TikTok Shop for impulse products and Shopify for their own storefront.

The Honest Cons

  • The 8% is really ~11-12%. Processing rides on the full order including shipping and tax, and the $0.30 flat fee stings on low-value orders.
  • Going live is the job. Shows run for hours, on schedule, with you talking. If you will not broadcast, the marketplace side works but loses the price-discovery premium that justifies the platform.
  • Tiers reset every four weeks. Miss a period's $15K and your rate snaps back to 8% - commission planning has a treadmill built in.
  • The category caps are promotions, not promises. The 0%-above-$1,500 caps are explicitly limited-time. Do not build a margin model on them.
  • Application gate. You cannot list and wait; sellers apply, get approved per category, and unapproved categories stay locked.

Bottom Line

Whatnot is the strongest live-selling venue open to ordinary sellers in 2026: no listing fees, an 8% headline commission that beats eBay's effective rate, and an audience that shows up to watch. The trade is labor - it rewards people willing to run real shows, and quietly taxes everyone else through processing fees and tier resets. It fits the marketplace row of the lineup in best AI tools for e-commerce.

Questions we get asked

What does Whatnot actually cost sellers?

Commission starts at 8% of the item price (shipping and tax excluded) plus 2.9% + $0.30 payment processing on the full order value - roughly 11-12% all-in for a typical order. There are no listing fees or subscriptions. Consistent 4-week sales drop the commission to 7.75% at $15K and down to 6.5% at $500K, with custom rates as low as 3% for invited sellers.

Do I need approval to sell on Whatnot?

Yes. Sellers apply through the seller application and are approved per category before going live. Approval gates exist because live selling carries audience trust; unapproved categories stay locked. The marketplace (buy-now) side also counts toward your tier once you are an approved seller.

Is the 0% commission above $1,500 on cards and coins permanent?

No - it is an explicitly limited-time promotion on select categories (comics, trading cards, sports singles, toys, coins). Do not build a margin model on it. The standing structure is 8% commission on item price plus payment processing, with tier discounts by 4-week volume.
