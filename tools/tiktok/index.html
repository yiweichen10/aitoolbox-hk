I have run a TikTok account alongside YouTube since 2023, and the summary I give everyone is this: TikTok is the best discovery engine on the internet and the worst-paying one. The Creator Rewards Program fixed part of that - it pays roughly 10 to 40 times more per view than the old Creator Fund - but the rules behind who gets paid are stricter than almost any other platform, and most people get surprised by them after they go viral, not before.

The Money Angle

Five income streams, in the order most creators actually earn from them:

  • Creator Rewards (the views-to-cash pipe). Original videos over 60 seconds earn roughly $0.40-$1.00 per 1,000 qualified views, with finance, business and tech niches reporting up to $1.50-2.00. The trap is the word qualified: views under five seconds, repeat watches, paid promotion traffic and anything outside the For You feed do not count, a video needs 1,000 qualified For You views before it earns a cent, and only videos you post *after* joining the program are eligible. A 15-second viral hit pays exactly zero.
  • Brand deals (the real money). For almost every creator this out-earns Creator Rewards by 3-10x. Market rates run roughly $50-200 per post at nano level, $200-1,000 at micro (10K-50K followers) and $5,000-15,000 at macro. Brands buy conversion, not impressions, so a 50K account with 8% engagement regularly out-charges a 200K account with 1%.
  • TikTok Shop affiliate. Tag products in videos and lives, earn 5-20% commission per sale (opens at 5,000 followers), with TikTok taking a 2.9% platform fee and paying biweekly. Demo products live and conversion runs 3-5x better than static links. This is the fastest stream to stack on top of content you were making anyway.
  • LIVE gifts. Go LIVE at 1,000 followers; viewers buy coins at about $0.0106 each and you keep roughly half of a gift's face value. Consistent live streamers frequently earn more from gifts than from Creator Rewards.
  • Series. Put up to 80 videos behind a paywall. Tutorials and behind-the-scenes content convert best - this is the closest thing TikTok has to selling your own course.

The Honest Cons

  • The math is brutal next to YouTube. The same million views that pays $1,500-5,000 in long-form YouTube ad revenue pays $400-1,000 here. The standard 2026 playbook is TikTok for discovery, YouTube for income.
  • Rules can change under you. The old Creator Fund paid $0.02-0.04 per thousand views before being shut down in the US, UK, Germany and France in late 2023. Program terms, RPM factors and eligible markets are all TikTok's to adjust.
  • Originality is judged by TikTok, not you. Subtitling someone else's clip, compiling, or lightly editing reposts fails the originality bar even when it feels like work. Faces, firsthand experience and a distinct argument are what pass.
  • AI slop has a target on it. Spammy, templated or mass-produced content risks losing qualified views retroactively - videos flagged later for violations can zero out a payout. AI-assisted work with real human input survives; raw generator exports do not.
  • Not available everywhere. If your market is not on the eligible list, Rewards is simply off the table - brand deals and Shop become your only routes.

Bottom Line

Treat TikTok as your top of funnel, not your paycheck. Post original 60-second-plus videos to feed Creator Rewards, but expect brand deals and Shop commissions to do the heavy lifting, and funnel the audience to YouTube or a newsletter where each view is worth five to ten times more. If your entire plan is "go viral and collect the views check," the qualified-view rules will make that plan smaller than it looks.

❓ FAQ

How much does TikTok actually pay per 1,000 views?

Roughly $0.40-$1.00 per 1,000 qualified views in 2026, with finance, business and tech content sometimes reaching $1.50-2.00. Two catches make real payouts smaller than the RPM suggests: only qualified views count (short watches, repeat plays, paid traffic and non-For-You views are excluded, so qualified views run below your visible count), and only original videos over 60 seconds posted after you join the program are eligible at all.

What do I need to qualify for Creator Rewards?

At least 10,000 followers plus 100,000 video views in the last 30 days, age 18 or older (19 in South Korea), a personal account - business accounts are excluded - in good standing, and residence in an eligible market such as the US, UK, Germany, France, Brazil, Japan or South Korea. Apply through TikTok Studio under monetization. The 100K views in 30 days hurdle is the one that filters out most new creators.

Can I make money with AI-generated videos on TikTok?

Carefully. AI-assisted content with real human input - your voice, your argument, your footage or editing - can qualify as original and keep earning. Templated, mass-produced generator output is exactly what TikTok's integrity systems target, and videos flagged for violations can lose their qualified views retroactively, zeroing out a payout. The originality bar is whether your audience could explain what you contributed; raw AI exports do not pass that test.
