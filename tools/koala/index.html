Where the money angle sits

This is the tool for programmatic niche sites. At Professional, 1.2M words/year is roughly 800-1,200 published articles - enough to stock several sites - for $348 a year; ad and affiliate revenue on one site that ranks covers that many times over. The live Amazon data (real reviews, prices, specs) is what makes product roundups credible instead of hallucinated. A second income stream: draft-for-hire services on Fiverr and Upwork, where Koala produces the first 80% and you edit the last 20%. Plan the keyword side first with our niche-site SEO stack and the keyword research comparison - content spend only pays when keywords were picked right.

The honest drawbacks

Drafts need human editing. Publishing Koala output raw is how sites get hit by scaled-content abuse policies - the successful operators edit, add original photos and cut weak sections. The word allowance is annual, not monthly: burn through 540k words by March on Starter and you wait for renewal. Model multipliers surprise people - writing with Opus at 3x burns a Starter plan in months. And the free tier is a one-time taste, not a rolling plan, so there is no long runway to judge output quality on your niche before paying.

Bottom line

Koala is the clearest yes among AI writers for affiliate publishers: cheap at entry, deep on SEO data, and proven by operator revenue screenshots rather than enterprise logos. Start with the free 5,000 words, edit ruthlessly, and upgrade to Professional only once a site earns. Current plans sit on the Koala pricing page.

Questions we get asked

Is Koala AI good for affiliate niche sites?

It was built for that exact use case. Live Amazon data feeds product roundups, outlines mirror current ranking pages, and internal linking is automatic. The operators quoted on the pricing page (Fatstacks, Minted Empire) run niche sites for a living. Drafts still need editing, original images and a human pass before publishing - treat Koala as the first 80%.

How does Koala compare to Jasper or ChatGPT?

Versus Jasper, Koala is cheaper at entry and has SERP and keyword data baked into drafting instead of separate add-ons. Versus raw ChatGPT, Koala runs per-article SERP analysis, pulls live Amazon listings and handles internal linking and WordPress publishing - things a chat window does not do.

What does the word limit actually mean?

Counts are quoted against the GPT-6 Luna baseline. If you generate with GPT-6.1 Sol or Claude Sonnet 5.5, every word counts double; Claude Opus 5.5 counts triple. A 540,000-word Starter allowance becomes roughly 270,000 words on Sonnet - plan your model choice around the allowance, not the other way round.
