Phrase Review 2026: What the Per-Word and Per-Seat Bill Really Costs, and How Agencies Resell Localization

Phrase is the enterprise localization platform most people knew as Memsource until the rebrand. You reach for it once a job is too big for a spreadsheet and a single translator, and you need governed machine translation across many languages and many reviewers. You connect repos, design files and support tools, and Phrase returns every language through its TMS, the Strings product built for software UI, or the Language AI layer that sits between your content and your customers.

I have watched agencies bill clients off Phrase and talked to teams that switched away because the bill turned unpredictable. Here is what the platform does in 2026, what the restructured pricing actually costs, and where a solo operator or small agency can turn it into recurring revenue.

What Phrase actually does

Phrase is two products under one roof. Phrase TMS is the workhorse: translation memory that reuses approved segments, terminology that keeps product names consistent, and QA checks that catch missing placeholders, broken tags and mixed punctuation before anything ships. Phrase Strings is the developer-facing side - it syncs software UI strings straight from GitHub, GitLab or a build pipeline so a new release ships in every language without a separate export step.

The layer that earns the 'AI' in its category is Phrase Language AI. AutoSelect picks the best machine-translation engine for each language and content type based on past performance. Quality Performance Score predicts segment quality before a human reviews it, so effort goes only where it adds value. Auto Adapt uses large language models to keep tone and brand voice. Next GenMT translates whole blocks instead of sentence by sentence, which reads more naturally and cuts post-editing. On top of that sit the AI Translation Agent and Phrase Custom AI, which trains on your own terminology.

Two releases matter for 2026. The MCP Server lets agents and LLMs pull translation memory and manage projects without writing custom API code, and the new Blackbird pack connects Phrase to 200+ enterprise apps including Notion, Airtable, Asana and ServiceNow. Zero-touch localization, in early access for Business and Enterprise on GitHub, detects a code change, triggers translation and returns finished strings to the codebase with no developer in the loop. Phrase Studio brings the same memory, style guides and quality scoring to video subtitles.

The 2026 pricing model

Phrase splits its plans into families, and the public page hides the top tiers behind 'custom'. The numbers below are verified against phrase.com/pricing in October 2026, billed annually, every plan with a 14-day trial and 24/7 support.

  • Freelancer (for solo linguists and LSPs): $27/month - 1 TMS seat, 200K managed words, 650K TMS processed words per year, 1M machine-translation units, 20K AI units.
  • Software UI/UX (the developer/designer plan): $525/month - 15 Strings seats, 1M managed words, 500K TMS processed words per year, 1M MT units, 10K AI units.
  • Professional (LSP): $525/month - unlimited TMS seats, 15 Strings seats, 300K paid plus 350K complimentary processed words per year, 2M MT units, 50K AI units.
  • Team (business): $1,245/month - unlimited TMS seats, 20 Strings seats, 1.2M managed words, 2.5M TMS processed words per year, 12M MT units, 25K AI units.
  • Business and Enterprise: custom quotes - 150+ Strings seats, millions of processed words, unlimited MT profiles and glossaries, SSO/SCIM/SLA.

Managed words are your source words multiplied by target languages. Processed words and AI units reset every year with no rollover, so a quiet quarter wastes budget and a busy launch eats the cap. AI units are the real variable cost: heavy AI Translation Agent or Custom AI use burns the annual bundle, after which you buy more or move up a plan. There is no permanent free tier; the floor is the $27 Freelancer plan.

How to make money with Phrase

The honest money here is not reselling the seat - it is selling the outcome. A 5,000-word app into 10 languages is 50,000 words of delivered content. Agencies I have spoken with invoice that at $4,000-6,000 for the first pass, then $300-1,500/month to keep it current on every release. Your tool cost on the Freelancer plan is $27/month, and even the $525 Software UI/UX plan leaves the margin intact because machine translation covers most UI strings.

Three plays work:

  • Localization as a service - take a client's product or help center, set up the connectors once, and bill a monthly retainer to keep every language in sync. Zero-touch localization lets you hand them a pipeline that runs itself after setup.
  • The LSP marketplace and partner program - Phrase lists language-service providers and runs a partner program, so you can stack reseller revenue on top of your service fee instead of competing on price.
  • Locale-launch sprints - a one-off $2,000-4,000 project to open a new market (app, site, docs, subtitles), with machine translation doing the bulk and a human touching only the high-stakes copy.

The catch is the same as with any localization tool: the margin is in the service, not the software. If you only translate a single landing page, the $27 floor plus your time still beats the $4,000-6,000 agency invoice, so be clear with clients about what they are paying for.

Who should pick Phrase

Choose Phrase if you run localization for more than one client, coordinate several translators or reviewers, and care about brand-controlled machine translation across large volumes. The Language AI orchestration, Custom AI and enterprise integrations are real differentiators, and the MCP Server makes it useful inside agent-driven workflows. Skip it if you want a free tier or a flat bill that never moves when you add languages - Crowdin and Lokalise handle those cases better, and DeepL covers pure translation without the project overhead.

Is there a free plan for Phrase?

No permanent free tier. Every plan opens with a 14-day trial and no credit card, but the cheapest paid plan is the $27/month Freelancer with a single TMS seat. If you need a free product to start a client job, Crowdin's free plan is the better entry point.

How does Phrase bill - by words, by seats, or by plan?

All three at once, which is why it trips people up. You pay a plan price that sets your Strings seats and managed-word cap, then usage is metered on TMS processed words and AI units that reset yearly. TMS seats are unlimited on paid plans, so the levers are words and AI, not seats. That is the inverse of Lokalise's flat plan-tier-with-unlimited-words model.

Can I make money with Phrase if I am not a translator?

Yes, and most people who do are not translators. Sell the outcome: a localized product or help center shipped on every release. A 5,000-word app into 10 languages invoices at $4,000-6,000 with a $300-1,500/month retainer, while your tool cost stays at $27-525/month. The LSP marketplace and partner program stack reseller revenue on top.
