Carrd builds one-page websites for $19 a year, and that price is not a trial gimmick - it is the regular Pro Standard plan, billed annually, with custom domains, SSL, forms and embeds included. Everything about the product follows from that constraint: one page, no bloat, fast load, and a fee structure that leaves your revenue alone.

Money Angle

  • The funnel page is the highest-ROI page on the internet. Affiliate offers, lead magnets and product launches all funnel through a single page with one job. Carrd is the cheapest infrastructure for that page anywhere - under $1.60/month at Pro Standard.
  • Checkout with zero platform cut. Pro Plus payment forms run Stripe Checkout with your own Stripe API keys, and Carrd's documentation lists no Carrd fee on those payments. Sell a $47 template and Stripe keeps 2.9% + $0.30; Carrd keeps nothing.
  • Email capture goes straight into tools you already pay for. Signup forms post to 20+ platforms - Kit, beehiiv, Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, MailerLite, Ghost, Buttondown - so the list stays yours, not Carrd's.
  • Link-in-bio on your own domain, without marketplace fees. Beacons gives you a free storefront but takes up to 9% of transactions on lower tiers; a Carrd page costs $19/year and never touches your revenue.
  • Ten sites on Pro Standard is a micro-agency. Landing pages for local businesses or fellow creators at $100-300 a page is a weekend-income classic, and your marginal cost is zero. For the storefront behind the page, Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy handle delivery.

The Honest Cons

  • One page means one page. No blog, no CMS, no product catalog, no real store. The moment you need structure, move to a full builder - the field is compared in best AI website builders.
  • No autosave. The most common Trustpilot complaint (3.1/5 overall) is losing edits - hit save constantly.
  • No AI anything. In an AI-tool directory this is the anti-feature: Carrd will not draft your copy or pick your layout. Pair it with a writing tool and bring your own headline.
  • Pro Lite is a trap for business use. $9/yr buys branding removal but not custom domains or forms - most real users need Pro Standard at $19/yr anyway.
  • Annual billing only. No monthly escape hatch if you need it for one launch month.

Bottom Line

Carrd is the utility player of this entire directory: it does not create content, it converts it. For $19 a year you get a page on your own domain that feeds your email list and checkout without taking a percentage of anything. If your monetization stack includes affiliate offers, a newsletter or digital products, the page they all run through costs less here than anywhere else.

Questions we get asked

Is Carrd really only $19 a year?

Yes - Pro Standard is $19 billed annually (about $1.58 a month) and includes custom domains with SSL, signup forms, widgets, embeds and analytics for up to 10 sites. There is no monthly billing. Your custom domain costs $10-15 a year separately at a registrar, and Pro tiers above Standard scale by site count ($39/yr for 25 sites on Standard, up to $599/yr for 1,000).

Can I sell products directly on a Carrd page?

Yes, on Pro Plus ($49/yr): payment forms run through Stripe Checkout with your own Stripe API keys, and Carrd's documentation lists no Carrd fee on those payments - you pay only Stripe's processing. Delivery is on you, though: most sellers pair the page with Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy for file delivery, and keep Carrd as the storefront.

Does Carrd have AI features?

No - no AI site generation, no AI copywriting, no layout suggestions. That is deliberate minimalism, but it means you bring the headline and the structure. The practical workflow is drafting copy with an AI writing tool and pasting it into Carrd, which keeps the $19/yr price possible.
