What AppSumo Actually Is

AppSumo is a launch marketplace for software and digital products. You apply; if accepted, your product goes in front of the company's 1.5M-subscriber email list with paid ads and 20,000 affiliates behind it. The economics are revenue share, not listing fees: you keep 95% of every sale from new AppSumo buyers and 70% from returning ones, and AppSumo keeps the rest. Campaigns run about 60 days. It is no longer purely a lifetime-deal shop - the official seller page states annual and other deal structures are negotiable.

The acceptance gate matters more than the fee table. About 10% of applicants get in, and the vetting criteria are blunt: a working product (not a roadmap), a team that will still exist in a year, and evidence you will handle support. The seller page explicitly welcomes AI tools and vibe-coded apps - "Built with Lovable, Bolt, Cursor or Replit? Fine, if it works and you'll support it" - which makes this one of the few credible distribution doors for a weekend-built Lovable or Bolt.new project that already has real users.

The Money Angle

Who actually makes money here: founders with a working SaaS or AI tool who need distribution more than margin. The official pitch: $113M+ paid to partners, roughly 40% of partner revenue comes from AppSumo emails, top performers get up to $25K of Google/Meta ad spend from AppSumo's pocket, and partners have raised $17B after launches - a launch as demand proof for investors.

The math to run before applying. A lifetime deal trades recurring revenue for a cash spike plus an audience. If your tool is $29/mo, one subscriber is worth $348/year; an AppSumo buyer paying $79 once is worth $79 - unless you convert them to a paid plan later. Founders who do well treat the launch as four assets at once: upfront cash for runway, a few thousand real users stress-testing the product, public reviews and case studies, and an email list of their own for upsells (AppSumo hands you the customer relationship, not just a payout). Founders who regret it are the ones whose entire model depended on month-4 recurring revenue with no upsell path.

Two doors, different muscle. The curated launch route (sell.appsumo.com) carries the full marketing machine described above. There is also a self-serve marketplace route (580+ products listed) with the same baseline split but lighter promotion - faster to get listed, quieter when it does.

The payout timing is the number people miss: you carry refund exposure for two months after a sale before the money is yours. Fine for a side project, painful if the launch is your only cash source.

The Honest Cons

First, rejection - roughly 90% of applications fail, and the "working product with support capacity" bar screens out exactly the pre-revenue projects that most want exposure. Second, the audience is deal-driven: Sumo-lings expect aggressive pricing, leave blunt reviews in public, and file support tickets at volumes that have overwhelmed solo maintainers. Third, a lifetime deal caps your earliest and most loyal users' payments forever - if your annual plan is $99 and your LTD is $79, your biggest fans paid you less than one year of subscription. Fourth, most AppSumo traffic is returning customers, so the 70% share, not the 95%, applies to the bulk of real volume. Fifth, the earnings claims on the sell page are based on the top 50% of partners per category; the median launch does not look like the case studies.

Selling direct through Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy keeps margin and pricing power but supplies zero audience - every buyer is one you brought yourself. AppSumo trades 5-30% of revenue plus deal-term control for a distribution engine you could not buy at retail. If you already have an audience, sell direct. If you do not, AppSumo is one of the few places an unknown tool gets real traffic on day one.

Bottom Line

AppSumo in 2026 is a distribution channel, not a business model. It fits when your product works, support will not break you, and you need users, reviews and runway cash more than you need recurring-revenue purity. It fails when your economics depend on subscription income you are about to sell at $79 for life. Apply with a working product, price the deal against your annual plan, and build an upsell path before launch day - the 95% share is real, and so are the 60-day wait and the 10% acceptance rate. Questions we get asked

Does AppSumo cost anything to list?

No. There are no upfront or listing fees on either the curated launch route or the self-serve marketplace; AppSumo takes its cut only from sales - 30% of returning-customer sales and 5% of new-buyer sales under the published baseline split (you keep 70% and 95% respectively). Rates are negotiable by category.

How much can you actually make on AppSumo?

AppSumo states $113M+ paid to partners in total and that about 40% of partner revenue comes from its emails. Its own earnings claims are based on the top 50% of partners per category over 12 months, so treat case studies as the ceiling, not the median. A well-priced tool with real utility to small-business owners converts the list; a niche B2B product with no support plan often stalls after the first email sends.

Is AppSumo still lifetime deals in 2026?

Not exclusively. The official seller page states AppSumo also works with annual pricing and other structures, negotiated per product. Lifetime deals remain the dominant format, but you can propose alternatives during the deal-terms step.

Can I sell an app I built with Lovable or Bolt?

Yes - the official sell page explicitly names Lovable, Bolt, Cursor and Replit builds as acceptable, provided the product is working and you will support it. Pre-launch or vaporware products are rejected.
