Money Angle

  • The freelance SEO stack under $60/month. Keyword research, rank tracking, backlink checks and site lookups for client work that bills $300-1,000 per client per month. One Basic plan carries two or three small local clients; Premium's +3 seats is where small teams stop sharing logins.
  • Whitelabel reporting is included. SERPWatcher reports and the Looker Studio connector turn rank data into client-facing dashboards with your branding - the deliverable that makes a retainer feel real in month one.
  • Local SEO sells by the city. 65k+ city-level locations mean you can quote "plumber keywords in Round Rock" with real volumes behind it - the easiest upsell in local consulting.
  • Niche-site keyword hunting. Low-difficulty long-tail research on a 2.5B-keyword database is the core loop described in best AI SEO tools for niche sites.
  • AI visibility reporting as a new line item. AI Search Watcher PRO (500 prompts) lets you sell "how visible is your brand in AI answers" alongside rank tracking - a service Otterly.AI buyers already pay $29+/mo for as a standalone.

Bottom Line

Mangools is the value pick of the SEO category: five tools, whitelabel reports, city-level local data and now AI-prompt tracking for roughly a quarter of suite money. The A/B pricing is annoying and the 48-hour refund window is strict - start on the free account, and pay annually the moment client work justifies it. Questions we get asked

Why do different reviews show different Mangools prices?

Mangools runs an A/B pricing test. Some visitors see AI-bundled plans (Basic $61/mo monthly or $37.70/mo annual), others see legacy non-AI plans ($49/$69/$129). Both sets are documented by trackers from Sep-Nov 2026; your checkout screen is the final number, so screenshot it.

Is there a free trial?

No timed trial. Mangools offers a free account with 5 keyword lookups per 24 hours (no card needed) and a 48-hour money-back guarantee on paid plans. The 48-hour window is short, so evaluate on the free account before paying.

Can I use Mangools for client work?

Yes - whitelabel reports and the Looker Studio connector are included for client-facing dashboards. Premium allows 3 extra seats and Agency 5, though the per-seat price is not published; ask support before planning a team around it.
