Medium is the only tool in this category where the platform hands you readers. Printify waits for your Etsy traffic, Kit waits for your email list, Kajabi waits for everything. On Medium you publish behind their metered paywall, paying members read, and you collect a slice of the membership pool. No clients to hunt, no products to build, no hosting bill, no storefront. For a writer with zero audience and zero budget, nothing else here gets you from blank page to paid story faster.

That same pitch is why most people earn almost nothing. Before you write a word, understand how the money actually moves in 2026, because the rules were rewritten six times in the last year.

How You Actually Get Paid

There is no rate per view. Medium pools its membership revenue and divides it by engagement, and only paying members generate money for you. A member read counts after 30 seconds on the page. Claps, highlights and replies from members feed into engagement points. Then comes the adjustment almost nobody mentions: Medium divides your 30-second-plus member reads by your total member views, and stories with an above-average ratio earn more. 10,000 non-member views from a Reddit link can pay less than 500 reads from actual subscribers. That single fact explains every confusing earnings report you have seen.

The model shifted monthly from late 2025, and every shift points the same direction - bring your own traffic:

Money Angle

Four paths, in order of what actually pays:

  • Write for members, in member-heavy niches. Personal finance, tech tutorials, productivity and self-improvement attract subscribers who read to the end. A 1,800-word piece read fully earns multiples of a skimmed listicle.
  • Write for search. Since November, member reads arriving from Google earn at a bonus rate funded by 15% of the entire payout budget. How-to posts, comparisons and evergreen tutorials sitting on real search queries now compound month after month - this is the closest thing Medium has to passive income.
  • Drive external traffic on purpose. External member reads earn a bonus on top, and every non-member your story converts into a subscriber pays a one-time fee. Cross-post links to LinkedIn, X and newsletters where your readers already are.
  • Use AI as a speed tool, never as the writer. Medium's AI policy bars fully AI-generated stories from the paywall, and AI-assisted work must be disclosed. The 2025 crackdowns targeted template-scaled AI slop specifically. First-hand experience is the moat: AI drafts, you supply the part only a human who lived it can write.

The Honest Cons

  • The rug has been pulled before. In early 2025 an algorithm change cut many established writers 70-90% overnight - community reports describe $500-800 months collapsing to $17-50. The program has been patched monthly since October 2025, but Medium's terms reserve the right to change or end it at any time, without notice.
  • Most writers earn very little. Community data puts the majority under $100/month, many at $1-20 even with consistent publishing. In Medium's last official earnings reports (2019-2020), only 6-9% of active writers ever crossed $100 in a payout period. The formula has changed several times since, the distribution has not.
  • The formula is opaque. No fixed rates, no published benchmarks, and a read-ratio adjustment you cannot see directly. Two stories with similar views can pay wildly different amounts and you will not learn why.
  • The audience is rented. Followers, distribution and the paywall all live inside Medium. There is no email export and no owned asset. This is a channel, not a business.

Bottom Line

Medium is real money with a short path in and a low ceiling for most people. Publish six genuinely useful stories in one niche, learn which titles pull search traffic, and treat every payout change as a signal to spread your writing across your own site and list. Earn your first $100 month here as proof the topic works - then take the audience knowledge somewhere you own. Do not build a rent-dependent income on a platform that rewrote its payout rules six times in one year.

❓ FAQ

How much does Medium actually pay per read or per 1,000 views?

There is no fixed rate and never has been. You earn a variable slice of the membership pool based on paying-member reading time, engagement points and a read-ratio adjustment. Views from non-members count for nothing. Realistic bands from community reports: most writers under $100/month, consistent writers with an audience $100-1,000+, and a small top tier $1,000-5,000+. In Medium's last official reports only 6-9% of active writers ever cleared $100 in a month.

Can I publish AI-generated articles and still earn?

Not if the article is fully AI-generated - Medium's AI policy bars that content from the paywall and Partner Program earnings. AI-assisted work is allowed but must be disclosed, and the 2025 crackdowns specifically targeted template-scaled AI slop. The practical rule: use AI to outline, draft and edit, but the story needs first-hand experience, real examples and your own judgment to be paywall-eligible and to survive curation.

Is the Medium Partner Program still worth joining in 2026 after the earnings crash?

Worth joining, not worth depending on. The program has actually improved since October 2025 - external and search traffic bonuses, fairer non-Boosted payouts, 100% writer cut since June 2026. But the 70-90% cuts of early 2025 proved the income is platform-controlled. Treat Medium as a testing lab and a first paying channel for your writing, keep your best work mirrored on a site you own, and never plan your rent around it.
