Where the money angle sits

Agencies sell this. A fixed-price onboarding and training pack - 15-25 narrated videos covering a client's core workflows - is a $1,500-5,000 product that takes a week in Guidde; the free 25-video tier covers exactly one such pack. Support leads embed guiddes in ticket replies and watch deflection in the analytics. SaaS teams convert their help PDFs into videos on Business without touching a video editor. The newer angle is agents: a guidde exports as a Claude agent skill, so documented processes execute rather than just play. Freelancers on Fiverr and Upwork list SOP documentation as a standalone gig - Guidde is the production line behind it.

The honest drawbacks

Business caps workspaces at 5 creators - a 12-person support team is Enterprise pricing with a sales call attached. The 25-video free cap is lifetime, not monthly, so you evaluate fast or pay. Add-on pricing opacity is a pattern: voice cloning, avatars, translation and MCP all cost extra at unpublished rates. Capture-only format limits reach - cinematic product demos with presenters still need Synthesia or HeyGen, and long-form editing needs Descript. Voiceovers in 50+ languages work for internal docs but still read synthetic on public marketing pages.

Bottom line

Guidde is the cheapest credible way to turn tribal knowledge into narrated, structured video documentation, and small enough to try on the free tier in one afternoon. Teams past five creators should get the Enterprise quote before committing to Business. Current plans sit on the Guidde pricing page.

Questions we get asked

What is Guidde used for?

How-to videos, SOPs and onboarding documentation. Record a workflow once; Guidde structures the steps, writes the narration and hosts a shareable page. Support teams embed guiddes in ticket replies to deflect repeat questions, and agencies package them as client training material.

Is there a free plan?

Yes - 25 how-to videos with recorded voiceover and share links, no credit card. The lifetime cap is the catch: once the 25 are used, you need Pro at $19/creator/mo (annual) for unlimited videos and watermark removal.

How is Guidde different from Loom or Scribe?

Loom records raw video and leaves the structure to you. Scribe makes screenshot-based step guides without narration. Guidde sits between them: structured steps, AI voiceover, captions, interactive click-through demos and analytics. Our process documentation comparison covers the full field.
