What PartnerStack Actually Is

PartnerStack is a Toronto company founded in 2015 that runs an affiliate network built exclusively for B2B software - and after a decade of doing only that, it has become the default. Over 600 SaaS companies run their partner programs on it, including Monday.com, Stripe, Freshworks, Pipedrive and ClickUp, with 115,000+ active partners on the marketplace and more than $3 billion in referred revenue behind it. In April 2026, AppDirect acquired the company - a signal of where the market thinks software distribution is heading.

The model is worth spelling out because it inverts what most people learn first. Amazon Associates and ClickBank pay you once per sale. PartnerStack programs mostly pay you every month your referral stays a paying customer - 30% of a $49/month subscription is $14.70 landing in your account each month, not a one-time $30 check. Affiliate veterans call this the difference between hunting and landlording, and it is the main reason B2B software affiliates out-earn retail affiliates with a fraction of the traffic.

It is also a curated network, not an offer dump. You join the network free, then apply to individual software programs, and each company reviews your site, your content and your traffic before approving you. That gate cuts both ways: junk offers stay out, and your conversion numbers look better because the programs you join match what your audience actually needs.

The Money Angle: Getting Paid Monthly for Work You Did Once

Five steps, in the order that actually works:

1. Build content before you apply. Every program application asks for your site URL, how you promote, and your traffic. Applications with three real published posts get approved; empty sites don't. Write the reviews first, then apply.

2. Join free and stack two or three matching programs. Pick tools your existing content already discusses. One dashboard, one payout pipeline, no per-program payment chasing.

3. Publish the three formats that convert in B2B: direct comparisons ("X vs Y"), roundups built for one specific job ("best X for agencies"), and how-to guides that name the tool. Readers arriving from these pages are already shopping - that intent is worth more than raw traffic.

4. Let the recurring math work. Refer one team to a $49/month tool on a 30% recurring, 12-month program: $176.40 from a single customer over the year. Ten customers across a quarter: about $1,764 in year-one commissions from content you wrote once. The same ten buyers through Amazon Associates at 4.5% on $60 orders pay $27 total, once.

5. Watch the AI-search layer. PartnerStack's CEO put it plainly in April 2026: the main driver of AI-search visibility in ChatGPT and Gemini is third-party content, and PartnerStack is the largest B2B content partner network in the world. Comparison posts no longer only convert humans - they teach the models which tool to recommend.

The category timing is real, too. PartnerStack's own 2026 roundup of AI affiliate programs (ClickUp, Gamma, MeetGeek, Surfer, Reclaim.AI, Castmagic and more) treats AI tools as the highest-commission shelf in the store. Reviewing AI software is precisely what sites like this one cover daily.

The Honest Cons

  • Money moves slowly by design. You earn in month one, the company reviews the month-end list in the first week of month two, and approved funds become withdrawable around the 13th. Add a 14-30 day B2B trial before a commission even exists, and your first withdrawal realistically lands 6-8 weeks after your first real conversion.
  • Every program is a separate job application. No blanket approval, no network-wide rate. New sites with thin content get rejected more often than the success-story posts admit, and reapplying costs time you could spend publishing.
  • "Recurring" usually has an expiry date. Most programs cap at 12-18 months. The word "recurring" in a marketplace listing is not a promise of forever - read the offer terms line by line before you project income.
  • Strictly B2B. If your audience is consumers, conversion will sit near zero. This is the wrong network for lifestyle, entertainment or general-interest niches, and no amount of enthusiasm fixes a mismatched audience.
  • You don't set the terms. Companies can decline commissions, place automatic fraud holds, or change their offer structure with a dashboard update. The AppDirect acquisition (April 2026) has changed nothing for partners so far, but your income is ultimately governed by companies you don't control.

Bottom Line

PartnerStack is the recurring-income piece of the affiliate puzzle that Amazon Associates and ClickBank leave empty: software subscriptions, the highest customer-lifetime-value shelf online, paying 20-40% monthly instead of once. Go in knowing three things. First, apply with real content or don't apply at all - the per-program gate is real and empty sites get filtered. Second, the payout cycle is slow: earn in month one, withdraw in month two, budget accordingly. Third, recurring almost always means 12-18 months, not forever - read every offer's cap before you do the math. If your content targets people who buy software for work, this is the highest-quality network you can join for free, and the $5 payout floor means nothing meaningful ever gets trapped.

❓ FAQ

How much can a beginner realistically earn in the first six months?

With a small site, three to five solid comparison pieces across two or three approved programs, $50-300/month by month six is a realistic band - lower if your traffic is under a few thousand monthly readers. The first withdrawal typically lands 6-8 weeks after your first conversion because commissions sit in 'Pending approval' until the company signs off at month-end. Anyone promising four figures in month one is selling a course, not a network.

Why is my commission stuck in 'Pending approval'?

That is the standard cycle, not a problem. Commissions accrue through the month as pending, the full list goes to the company at month-end, and they approve or decline each one; approved funds become withdrawable around the 13th. B2B adds delays: 14-30 day trials, refund windows, and some programs release commissions as 'Scheduled' payments in increments. If a commission sits unusually long, message the program directly from your dashboard's Messages tab.

Can you earn on PartnerStack without a big audience?

Yes, because B2B intent beats audience size. Five hundred monthly readers searching for 'best CRM for small teams' will out-convert 50,000 casual followers. What you cannot have is nothing: programs review your site and content plan before approving you, so publish first. A focused comparison site, a niche newsletter, or a YouTube channel with real reviews is enough to get approved.
