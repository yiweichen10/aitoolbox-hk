What Teachable Actually Is

Teachable is a dedicated course platform: hosting, checkout, student management and a clean course player, with a free plan that lets you publish one real course and sell it before you pay Teachable anything. The trade is the fee ladder: the free plan takes $1 + 10% of every sale, Basic takes 5%, and Pro takes 0%. Where Kajabi bundles email, funnels, community and a website into a higher-priced suite, Teachable does course delivery well and stops there - you bring your own email tool (Kit or beehiiv), your own pages and your own community.

It sits in the middle of the field this site tracks: marketplaces like Udemy bring students but take pricing control and pay 37% on market sales; subscription pools like Skillshare pay by watch minutes; Gumroad sells files with no course structure. Teachable is for people who own their audience and want a storefront they control at a mid-market price.

The Money Angle

Step 1 - validate on free. Publish one course, connect Teachable Payments (Stripe-powered, 130+ countries), price it as a one-time payment or payment plan. The $1 + 10% fee stings but costs nothing monthly: a $97 sale nets you about $83 while you find out whether anyone buys.

Step 2 - upgrade on data, not hope. The fee math: free-to-Basic pays for itself around $780/month in course revenue; Basic-to-Pro around $2,380/month. Below those lines, stay put and spend the difference on audience building.

Step 3 - raise average order value. Bundles, order bumps and payment plans are native. A $47 course with a $97 bundle option routinely out-earns two separate courses, and payment plans convert buyers who cannot justify the lump sum.

Step 4 - affiliates at Pro. The affiliate program (Pro and up) lets students and partners promote for a cut. This is how course businesses grow past their own email list - and why the Pro tier is the first one that behaves like a business plan rather than a store.

The honest part: Teachable is a delivery engine, not a traffic source. People making real money here already have an audience from YouTube, a newsletter, SEO or social. If you do not, build that first - see how to make money with AI tools.

The Honest Cons

The free plan's $1 + 10% is the worst deal in the category at volume - Thinkific's free plan charges 0% on its one course. There is no email marketing, no community space and no real landing page builder, so every serious Teachable business pairs it with an email tool (Kit is the common pick; see our email marketing comparison) - which narrows the price gap to Kajabi once you add the second subscription. Certificates, affiliates and graded quizzes only arrive at Pro. And the free plan's single-course cap blocks multi-product testers.

Bottom Line

Teachable is the right pick when you have an audience, want the lowest-risk way to sell a real course, and accept that email and community live elsewhere. Validate on free, move to Basic when the 10% bleed exceeds $39/month, and go Pro when affiliates and certificates matter. If you want everything in one box and will actually use it, compare Kajabi; if you want marketplace traffic with no list-building, compare Udemy. Pair the course with an AI content stack - see our creators' toolkit - and the platform itself becomes the smallest line in your budget.

Questions we get asked

Is Teachable's free plan actually usable?

Yes, with one course and unlimited students - a real way to validate demand at $0 monthly. The catch is per-sale: a $97 course sale costs $10.70 to Teachable plus about $3.20 in Stripe fees, so you keep roughly $83. That is acceptable while testing and expensive at volume; fee trackers put the break-even for upgrading to Basic (5% fee, $39/mo annual) around $780/month in sales.

How does Teachable compare to Kajabi?

Kajabi bundles email marketing, funnels, community and a website builder into one subscription at a higher price (see our Kajabi review); Teachable focuses on course delivery at a lower cost and expects you to bring your own email tool such as Kit or beehiiv. If you will actually use Kajabi's bundled features, the price gap narrows; if you just need to sell a course well, Teachable is cheaper. The full three-way comparison is in our course platform guide.

What does Teachable take from a $100 sale?

Free plan: $11.00 to Teachable + about $3.20 Stripe = you keep roughly $85.80. Basic: $5.00 + $3.20 = about $91.80. Pro and up: $0 platform fee + $3.20 = about $96.80. The plan you should be on is the one where the subscription costs less than the fees it removes.
