CURRENT PRICING
Free to join · $8-$12/hr floor
Participant side is free. Prolific enforces a minimum reward of £6/$8 per hour with £9/$12 recommended (verified from official researcher pricing pages, 2026-11-22). Rates are computed on median actual completion time; underpaying studies get flagged and researchers have 22 days to correct them, often via adjustment bonuses. Participants receive the full reward plus bonuses; custom screener time is paid even when you are rejected. Payouts go via PayPal in the study's currency (GBP or USD). Researchers pay a 33.3% (academia/non-profit) or 42.857% (corporate) platform fee on top of rewards, which is where the money in the system comes from.

Prolific is the academic-grade way to get paid for your opinions, and in 2026 it has become one of the quiet backdoors into the AI economy: universities and AI labs run studies here to collect human judgment for training and evaluating models. Two things separate it from the survey-app sprawl. First, real cash in GBP or USD through PayPal - no points, no gift-card maze. Second, an enforced pay floor: researchers must pay at least $8 per hour, and Prolific's own recommendation is $12.

Prolific publishes its reward rules on official help pages, and they are unusually participant-friendly:

That last row is the sleeper feature. On most survey walls, screen-outs are unpaid grief. On Prolific, researchers are charged for screener time and you get paid a little for it.

Money Angle

  • Treat it as a cash floor, not a career. The floor is $8-12/hr while you are inside a study. Realistic monthly output from steady participants runs $40-100 (third-party earnings surveys), with casual users closer to $10-30. That is phone-bill money that requires no audience, no product and no interview.
  • The AI angle is real: your profile is the product. AI developers use Prolific to source human feedback and annotated data - the same demand that pays $15-50/hr on Outlier, just in smaller academic-sized chunks. Filling in education, work history and specialist skills routes you into those higher-paying studies.
  • Complete every profile section honestly and fully. Studies target narrow segments. A thorough profile is the difference between three invitations a week and three a day.
  • Stack it with the other two chairs. The working combo most people run is Prolific (steady, lower pay) plus UserTesting ($10 per 20-minute test) plus User Interviews ($50-150 per session, rare but big). Prolific fills the gaps between the other two.
  • Cash out and keep records. Payments land via PayPal in the study's currency. It is taxable income in most countries; export your history monthly.

The Honest Cons

  • The floor is also the ceiling for most studies. General-population studies cluster at minimum-to-recommended rates. The $12/hr promise applies while you work, not while you wait for work.
  • Study supply is demographic roulette. Students, US/UK residents and common age bands see the most studies; if you rarely match, weeks can go quiet.
  • It is income smoothing, not income replacing. Nobody supports themselves on Prolific alone; its role is the reliable filler between better-paying platforms like Outlier or Mercor.
  • PayPal dependency. If PayPal does not operate in your country, getting paid is complicated.

Bottom Line

Prolific is the lowest-friction legitimate cash platform in this directory: no microphone, no interview, no portfolio - and the only one with a published, enforced pay floor. Run it as the filler between UserTesting and User Interviews, complete your profile properly, and let the AI-training studies find you. Questions we get asked

How much can I realistically earn per month on Prolific?

Steady participants report roughly $40-100 a month, casual users closer to $10-30 (third-party earnings surveys, 2026). The $8-12/hr rate applies while you are inside a study, not while you wait for one - so your effective hourly across a whole session is lower. Specialist profiles (degree fields, technical skills) route you into higher-paying AI-training studies.

Why do I keep getting screened out - and do those minutes count?

Studies target narrow segments, so rejections are normal. On Prolific the screener economics favor you: time spent in a custom screener is paid (a small amount) even when you are rejected, because the researcher is charged for it. Pre-screeners answered once in your profile cost you nothing.

Is Prolific available in my country?

The participant pool spans 38 countries with studies published in GBP or USD. The US and UK see the deepest study supply; other eligible countries get fewer but real invitations. You need a way to receive PayPal payouts in GBP or USD. Anyone charging you a signup or training fee is impersonating Prolific - the platform is free to join.
