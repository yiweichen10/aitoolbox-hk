When a buyer asks ChatGPT 'which X should I use' and your brand never appears, that is traffic you cannot see in any analytics suite. Profound built the measurement layer for that problem: structured prompts run daily across AI engines, tracking where your brand shows up, which sources get cited, how sentiment shifts, and which competitors own which questions. Its Agent Analytics then connects the dots to actual traffic through CDN and analytics integrations. The customer list is the enterprise proof - Ramp, DocuSign, Target, Walmart, Samsung and Figma by the company's own account, on a Series C raised in early 2026.

Pricing: two doors, a narrow middle

The official page today offers a free Trial and a custom Enterprise plan, nothing in between. Trial: 50 unique prompts run daily for 7 days across ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews, one language and region, Agent Analytics on unlimited domains, Agents and Sheets included - but no exports, API or SSO, and you cannot customize the prompt set. Enterprise is quote-only: up to 9 answer engines (adding Perplexity, Copilot, Claude, DeepSeek and Google AI Mode), tailored prompt plans, the full Prompt Volumes dataset, Agents at scale, CSV/JSON exports, all-time history, SSO/SAML and SOC 2, with unlimited seats. Third-party reviews from June 2026 documented self-serve Starter at $99/mo annual (ChatGPT only, 50 prompts, one seat) and Growth at $399/mo (three engines, 100 prompts, three seats); reported Enterprise contracts run $2,000-5,000+ per month. Verify current tiers on the official page - the self-serve grid may have moved.

How agencies make money with it

GEO retainers are the monetization story. The pattern that works: run the free trial on a prospect's brand, walk into the pitch meeting with data they have never seen (how ChatGPT misdescribes their product, which competitor owns their category questions), and price the fix as a monthly retainer at $1,500-5,000 - one client covers the Enterprise seat cost with margin. Inside the retainer, Prompt Volumes drives the content roadmap (real question demand, not keyword guesses), Agent Analytics proves AI-sourced traffic growth in quarterly reports, and Agents/Sheets automate the re-measurement. Smaller shops without enterprise clients replicate the service with Otterly.AI at $29-189/mo or Peec AI, monitoring breadth for the price of one Profound day. The honest trade-offs

The product everyone benchmarks lives behind a quote - accessible tiers cover a fraction of the engines, and June 2026's annual-only billing removed the monthly escape hatch. The 7-day trial is short against the 1-3 months realistic teams need before acting on the data, and G2 reviewers have documented citation counts diverging from manual ChatGPT spot-checks - validate before the annual commitment. If you need broad engine coverage at low cost rather than the deepest dataset, rivals beat it on price; if you need to prove AI-visibility ROI to a board, nothing else has the data depth.

Questions we get asked

Is Profound worth it for a small site?

Almost never. The accessible tiers are narrow (ChatGPT-only at the documented $99 Starter) while the headline features need an Enterprise quote in the low thousands per month. A small site that wants multi-engine monitoring for $29 should start with Otterly.AI or Peec AI, and the whole field is laid out in our AI search visibility comparison.

What is Prompt Volumes and why does it matter?

An opt-in panel dataset of the real questions people type into AI engines, broken down by region and demographic. Other GEO tools tell you how your brand appears for prompts you chose; Prompt Volumes shows the demand side - which questions exist at all and how big they are. The full searches panel is Enterprise-only.

What does the free trial actually include?

50 unique prompts run daily for 7 days across ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews, in one language and region, with Agent Analytics on unlimited domains, plus Agents, Sheets and analytics integrations. No exports, API or SSO, and prompts are a recommended set you cannot customize on Trial.
