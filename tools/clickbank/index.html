What ClickBank Actually Is

Running since 1998 out of Boise, Idaho, ClickBank is the oldest large affiliate marketplace for digital products - courses, software, supplements, e-books - and it still pays more per sale than almost any network alive: $7.5B+ sent to affiliates with zero missed payments since 1999. Sign-up is free and instant, no application queue. Browse the marketplace, hit Promote on an offer, and a HopLink lands in your clipboard. The 60-day cookie means a click today can still pay you two months out.

The reason commissions here hit 50-75% (90% for whitelisted partners) while Amazon pays 3%: the products are digital, cost nothing to duplicate, and the vendor still keeps roughly half of every sale they would never have made without you. One $60 sale at 60% is about $32 - more than fifteen Amazon book commissions combined.

How People Actually Make Money With It

  • One good sale beats a thousand clicks. Average payouts on top health and make-money offers run $40-$200 per conversion (reported). A review page that converts one visitor in fifty earns more per thousand visits than most AdSense sites see in a month. AI tools draft the comparisons, the email sequences, the ad variants - the part that still needs a human is picking offers you would actually buy.
  • Gravity is the only metric that matters at first. It counts distinct affiliates who made at least one sale in the past 12 weeks, weighted toward recent activity. Above roughly 100 means the product converts but is saturated; the reported sweet spot is 10-80, where the offer sells and you are not competing with 500 media buyers. The marketplace shows average dollars per conversion and refund trends before you commit to anything.
  • Email funnels are where the repeat money lives. AI-drafted sequences through Kit or beehiiv, one solid offer, and the cookie window barely matters - the link sits in a list you own.
  • Know the verticals you are walking into. Weight loss, supplements, survival, forex education - that is where the payouts are fattest, refund rates run 10-25% on some offers (community-reported), and FTC liability for health claims lands on the promoter, not just the vendor. Repeating a bogus claim in your own words is your lawsuit too.

Bottom Line

ClickBank pays the highest commissions a beginner affiliate can reach with zero approval friction, and the 60-day cookie plus public conversion stats make it the cleanest place to learn the trade. The trade-off is payout mechanics built for distrust: a five-sale entry gate, a $5-per-payment fee that quietly taxes small thresholds, a 10% reserve held for 12 weeks, and a dormant fee ladder that eats forgotten balances. The marketplace's biggest strength - fat commissions on aggressive direct-response offers - is also where refund rates, FTC exposure, and burned audiences live.

Money angle summary: pick offers in the 10-80 gravity band, sanity-check the refund trend before promoting, draft content and email sequences with AI but buy every product you recommend, set your payment threshold near $500 so the $5 fee stops mattering, treat every commission as provisional for 60 days, and never let the account sit dormant with money in it.

❓ FAQ

Why can't I withdraw money when my dashboard shows a balance?

Three separate gates. First, the Customer Distribution Requirement: 5 sales from unique customers, each more than 14 days old, spread across at least 2 payment methods - five Visa sales do not count, and it is the single most common reason new affiliates see a frozen balance. Second, the payment threshold (default $100, adjustable down to $10). Third, the 10% reserve, which sits for about 12 weeks to cover refunds. The CDR is a one-time gate; after you clear it, payouts run normally.

What does a ClickBank sale actually pay?

Commissions are computed after ClickBank's 7.5% + $1 cut: a $100 product at 60% commission pays $54.90, not $60. Marketplace offers typically run 50-75% (up to 90% for whitelisted affiliates), and average payouts on top health and make-money offers are reported at $40-$200 per conversion - which is why one ClickBank sale can outearn a dozen Amazon sales.

Is ClickBank still legit in 2026, or a scam marketplace?

The platform is legitimate: merchant of record since 1998, $7.5B+ paid with zero missed payments since 1999, in-house compliance review of every listing, and US sales tax plus EU VAT remitted for you. The offers vary wildly. Aggressive supplement and get-rich funnels still dominate the top-gravity lists. Promote products you would buy yourself, check the refund trend before scaling, and remember the FTC holds affiliates responsible for the claims they repeat, not just the vendor who wrote them.
