What Comet Does Well

The standout feature of Comet is its natural language processing. You don't have to click through menus to set a deadline or assign a priority level. You just type, "Remind me to call the client about the contract on Thursday at 2 PM," and the tool handles the rest. It's smart enough to know that "Thursday" means the upcoming one and "2 PM" is a specific time slot. This saves those precious seconds that usually get lost in the UI of more bloated tools.

Another win is the AI task prioritization. Most of us struggle with the "everything is urgent" trap. Comet looks at your deadlines, the estimated time to complete a task, and your existing calendar commitments to tell you what to focus on. It creates a "Suggested Flow" for your day. If it sees that you have a big project due on Friday, it'll nudge you to start the heavy lifting on Tuesday morning when you're usually most productive.

The calendar integration is tight. It doesn't just sit next to your Google or Outlook calendar; it lives inside it. When you add a task, Comet looks for "Focus Blocks" in your schedule. If you have a two-hour gap between meetings, it will suggest a task that fits that specific window. This prevents the "I only have 30 minutes, I can't start anything big" excuse. It fills the gaps in your day with meaningful progress.

Lastly, the smart scheduling suggestions are a lifesaver for anyone who hates manual planning. If a meeting gets moved, Comet automatically shifts your tasks to the next available slot. You don't have to go in and reschedule everything yourself. This automation takes a huge cognitive load off your shoulders. You can trust that the tool is looking at the big picture so you can focus on the specific task in front of you.

Comet review: Pricing and Plans

When it comes to the cost, Comet keeps things straightforward. The Free tier is surprisingly generous. It gives you access to the core AI task creation and basic prioritization features. For students or solo creators who are just starting out, this is often enough to get a taste of how the system works. You get the mobile and desktop apps, plus integration with one primary calendar.

The Pro plan sits at $15 per month. This is where the tool really starts to show its muscles. At this level, you get unlimited calendar syncs, advanced AI scheduling that learns your productivity patterns over time, and priority support. It also unlocks "Deep Work Mode," which silences notifications and helps you stay in the zone. If you’re a professional whose time is worth more than a few dollars an hour, the $15 investment pays for itself within the first week of improved focus.

For larger organizations, the Team plan offers custom pricing. This version includes collaborative features like shared project boards, team-wide priority alignment, and admin controls. It’s built for teams that want to ensure everyone is working on the highest-impact tasks. The AI can even analyze team-wide bottlenecks to suggest better resource allocation. You have to talk to their sales team for a quote, which is standard for enterprise-level software.

Comparing the value, Comet positions itself as a premium productivity partner. While $15 might seem high for a "to-do list," it’s better to think of it as a scheduling assistant. If it saves you even one hour of manual planning a month, it has already covered its cost. The simplicity of the pricing model is a plus—no hidden fees or complicated add-ons to worry about.

Limitations Worth Knowing

No tool is perfect, and Comet has its share of growing pains. Because it’s a newer player in the market, it has a smaller user base than giants like Notion or Trello. This means you won’t find as many community-made templates or third-party integrations. If your entire workflow relies on a specific niche tool that doesn’t have a Comet integration yet, you might find yourself doing some manual data entry.

The learning curve is another factor. While the natural language input is intuitive, getting the AI to prioritize tasks exactly how you like them takes a bit of time. You have to "train" the tool by giving it feedback on its suggestions. For the first few days, it might suggest a task for a time when you’d rather be taking a break. You need to be patient while it learns your specific rhythm.

Integrations are currently a bit thin compared to the industry leaders. If you want to pull tasks directly from specialized software like Jira or GitHub, you might find the options limited. While it handles the big names like Google and Slack well, the "long tail" of software integrations is still being built. For power users who have a deeply complex tech stack, this could be a dealbreaker.

Lastly, some users might find the AI a bit too "opinionated." If you’re someone who likes to have total manual control over every minute of your day, the automated scheduling might feel restrictive. The tool is designed to take the decision-making out of your hands, but that’s only a benefit if you trust its logic. If you find yourself constantly overriding the AI's suggestions, you’re not getting the full value of the tool.

FAQ

Is Comet AI free?

Yes, Comet offers a free tier that includes basic task creation and calendar integration. It's a great way to test the tool's natural language features without spending a dime.

What is Comet AI used for?

Comet is primarily a task management and scheduling tool. It uses AI to help you decide what to focus on by looking at your deadlines and calendar.

Is Comet better than Notion AI?

It depends on your needs. If you need a full workspace for notes and databases, Notion AI is hard to beat. But if you specifically want a tool that manages your schedule and tells you exactly when to do each task, Comet is much more specialized and effective for that purpose.

How does Comet AI work?

Comet works by connecting to your digital calendars and using natural language processing to understand your tasks. When you type a task, it analyzes its urgency and fits it into the empty slots in your schedule. It continuously updates your plan based on your progress and any changes to your calendar.
