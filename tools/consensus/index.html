Money Angle

  • Cited content rates higher. Writers who back claims with real studies charge more and get cited more - Consensus turns "trust me" into footnote-able evidence. Pair it with Surfer SEO when a client brief demands both structure and sources.
  • Sell research support, not ghostwriting. Deep Reviews digest dozens of papers into a synthesized brief - consultants and academic-support services resell that as paid literature-review work, with the ethics line held at summaries and editing.
  • Newsletters with receipts. Evidence-based niche newsletters (health, finance, longevity) live or die on citations; the free 10 messages a month cover a weekly issue's fact-checking without a subscription.
  • MCP changes the workflow. Claude or ChatGPT can query Consensus directly through MCP, so citations flow into drafts automatically instead of being hunted down manually - a quiet edge for content teams.
  • The corpus is the moat. Answers come only from peer-reviewed work, so the output survives scrutiny in front of a client, a journal reviewer or opposing counsel.

The Honest Cons

  • Deep Reviews are the headline feature and they are rationed. 15 a month on Pro disappears fast during a real literature sweep; real volume means $540/yr.
  • Peer-reviewed-only is a bias. Preprints, industry reports and non-English research get thin coverage - emerging topics can return nothing useful.
  • Read the abstracts anyway. The citation graph is real; the AI synthesis is still AI. Repeating a claim to a client without opening the source is how credibility dies.
  • The discount is a process, not a toggle - students, faculty and US clinicians verify through email or NPI, and the 40% is an upper bound.
  • Monthly billing stings. $20/$65 against the $12/$45 annual rates is a 40-67% premium for flexibility.

Questions we get asked

Can students use Consensus for coursework?

Yes, as a search and synthesis aid - and it is the safer AI choice because every answer cites the underlying studies, so verification is quick. It is a research tool, not a ghostwriter; institutional AI policies still apply. Students and faculty with a school email get up to 40% off paid plans.

What is the difference between a Pro message and a Deep Review?

A Pro message is a dynamic AI search and analysis of your question across the paper database. A Deep Review is the heavier product: a comprehensive, structured literature review of many studies. Quotas run 15 Deep Reviews a month on Pro and 200 on Deep; Pro messages are unlimited on both paid tiers.

Is the free plan usable long-term?

For light use, yes: 10 Pro messages, up to 3 Deep Reviews and basic paper search every month, plus API and MCP access to try. A weekly newsletter's fact-checking fits inside the free quota; daily client research does not.
