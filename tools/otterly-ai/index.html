What Otterly.AI Actually Is

Otterly.AI is a monitoring tool for a question that did not exist two years ago: when someone asks ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google's AI Overviews, AI Mode, Copilot or Claude about something you sell, does your brand come up, what does the answer say about you, and which sources does the model cite? You build a prompt set (or generate one with the built-in Prompt Research and Keywords-to-Prompts tools), Otterly checks those prompts daily against the engines you picked, and it scores visibility, sentiment and Share of Voice while logging every citation.

It is measurement, not construction - the same honest boundary that applies to Peec AI and every other tool in our AI search visibility comparison. What it changes is who can afford to answer the question: at $29/month it is the floor of a market where comparable rigor starts near $85-99.

The Money Angle

GEO retainers are the service of 2026. Businesses watched their traffic charts bend and now ask agencies two questions: "are we visible in AI answers?" and "why not?" A defensible retainer workflow is: set 15-30 prompts per client, track daily, report visibility/sentiment/citations monthly, then pair the findings with content work in Surfer SEO or NeuronWriter to actually fix the gaps. Otterly's cost per client sits between $29 (Lite, one small brand) and a slice of $189 (Standard, several clients); GEO retainers in this space are commonly quoted at $500-2,000/month. The tool is not the service - the reporting cadence and the fixes are - but the $29 floor is what makes the margin work at small-client scale.

The audit door-opener. Every plan includes unlimited GEO URL audits. A free-looking audit ("here's what ChatGPT says about your category, and it cites your competitor") is the cheapest discovery call material in the SEO world right now. Run the audit, show the citation gap, sell the retainer.

Where the retainer math breaks. Do not sell "we'll get you into ChatGPT" as a guarantee. These tools report positions in a probabilistic, personalizing system; honest contracts sell measurement plus optimization effort, not ranks. Agencies that promise outcomes get churned clients.

The Honest Cons

The entry tier is a single-brand tool wearing an agency badge: 16-20 daily prompts cannot meaningfully cover two competitive niches, and credit packs start at Standard, so a Lite user who needs more can only jump five tiers in price. Claude's 3-credit pricing quietly triples the cost of the one engine where citation behavior differs most. And the deepest limitation is categorical: Otterly tells you where you stand and which sources get cited, but it will not write the pages, fix the schema, or negotiate the placement - expect to pair it with content and technical work, and budget for that honestly in any retainer quote.

Bottom Line

Otterly.AI is the tool to learn GEO reporting on and the tool to run small-client GEO retainers on: $29 buys real daily data across the engines that matter, unlimited reporting structure, and audits you can sell with. Its ceiling is honest - it measures, it does not fix - so the money is in pairing it with content work and billing for the whole loop, not the dashboard. Start on Lite with one brand, prove the reporting rhythm, and upgrade only when prompt volume, not ambition, forces it.

Questions we get asked

Does Otterly.AI have a free plan?

No permanent free plan. There is a 7-day free trial that requires no credit card, and after it ends you pick a paid plan - Lite at $29/month is the entry point. Annual billing on any plan saves 15%.

What exactly is one credit?

One credit is one prompt checked once on one engine. Claude costs 3 credits per check; every other engine costs 1. Daily tracking means each prompt-engine pair is checked 31 times a month, so one prompt tracked daily across 4 engines uses 124 credits per month. Your plan's credit balance (2,000 on Lite up to 50,000 on Premium) sets how many prompt-engine combinations you can sustain; credits do not carry over month to month.

Can an agency track multiple clients on one subscription?

Yes - every paid plan includes unlimited workspaces, brands, competitors, team members and countries, and engines are configured per workspace. The practical limit is the prompt count, not the org structure: Lite handles one or two small clients, Standard's 120-160 daily prompts covers a handful of accounts, and agencies running 10+ client workspaces usually land on Premium. For client-facing data delivery, Standard's API and Looker Studio connector feed dashboards directly.
