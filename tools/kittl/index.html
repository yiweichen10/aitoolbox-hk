Kittl is a browser design studio built around print-on-demand work: vector-friendly templates, curved text, AI image generation from dozens of models, bulk mockups and print-ready exports in one tab. Where Canva AI aims at marketing teams and Recraft at vector-first AI, Kittl aims at the seller who wants a design on an Etsy listing tonight - and it grants a full commercial license on every plan, including the free one.

What the plans actually cost

Verified on kittl.com/pricing, 2026-11-28 - note the page prices in EUR (a currency toggle exists; the figures below are the EUR we read). Free: EUR 0 with a one-time 100 AI tokens (up to about 20 images), 5 saved projects, 500 MB storage, standard exports, full commercial license and unlimited bulk mockups. Pro: EUR 15/mo monthly or EUR 12/mo annual - 2,000 tokens/mo plus daily bonus tokens, unlimited projects, 10 GB, high-resolution exports, premium models, brand kit. Expert: EUR 35 monthly / EUR 26 annual - 6,000 tokens/mo, 200 daily bonus tokens, 100 GB. Max: EUR 65 monthly / EUR 32.50 annual for the first year (Black Friday 50% off, code BF2026; renews EUR 48/mo) - 12,000 tokens/mo, 600 daily bonus tokens, 1 TB. Token spending is published per model: Expert covers roughly 600 Seedream 5.0 Lite images, 250 Nano Banana 2 images or 37 Veo 3.1 videos per month.

Where the money angle sits

POD sellers are the obvious fit, and the free plan works as a first-shop studio: 20 sellable designs plus unlimited mockups for EUR 0, pushed to Printify, Printful, Redbubble or Amazon Merch on Demand. Expert at EUR 26/mo annual is the working volume tier - 1,200 images/mo is a full seasonal catalog refresh. The second angle is service work: logo and brand kits for local businesses (see our logo tool comparison) or listing design packs sold to other POD sellers. Template-plus-AI beats a blank artboard when the client has EUR 200, not EUR 2,000.

The honest drawbacks

The free plan is a demo, not a workspace: 100 tokens is one afternoon of generation, and 500 MB storage fills in a week. EUR-default pricing means USD cards see different totals at checkout, and the page we read showed no USD figures - budget with the EUR rates. Video token burn is steep, and the Max discount is first-year only (renews at EUR 48/mo). It is browser-only with no offline mode, and heavy artboards lag on older machines. For photo manipulation and product-photo cleanup, Photoroom still wins - Kittl's strength is graphic and typographic POD work.

Bottom line

Kittl is the fastest route from idea to sellable POD listing, and the commercial license on Free makes trying it a EUR 0 decision. Work on the free plan until token limits bite, then Expert annual is the sensible home. Current plans sit on the Kittl pricing page.

Questions we get asked

Can I sell designs made on the Kittl free plan?

Yes. Kittl grants a full commercial license on every plan including Free, and mockups are unlimited even there. The practical limits are export resolution and the one-time 100 AI tokens - enough for a first batch of listings, not for ongoing catalog work.

How does Kittl compare to Canva?

Kittl targets print-on-demand and vector work: curved text, print exports, transparent backgrounds and bulk mockups. Canva is broader marketing design with a bigger template library but weaker POD specifics. Many sellers run both - Canva for social posts, Kittl for products. See also Recraft if vector-first AI generation matters more than templates.

What are AI tokens and how far do they go?

Each model consumes tokens at a published rate. On Expert (6,000 tokens/mo) that is roughly 600 Seedream 5.0 Lite images, 250 Nano Banana 2 images, 120 Nano Banana 2 4K images, or 37 Veo 3.1 videos per month. Daily bonus tokens (200 on Expert, 600 on Max) sit on top of the monthly grant.
