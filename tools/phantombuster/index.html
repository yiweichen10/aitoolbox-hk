Money Angle

  • Lead-gen as a service is the classic wrapper. The pipeline everyone sells: extract a LinkedIn or Sales Navigator search (~12 minutes per 1,000 leads), enrich with emails, push to the client's CRM, repeat weekly. One Grow plan runs that across a book of clients from a single workspace - and 100 members means the whole agency shares capacity without per-seat fees.
  • Chain it into the outreach stack. Phantoms feed Clay for waterfall enrichment, then hand off to Instantly or Smartlead for sending - the cold email comparison covers the sending half.
  • Sell intent lists, not bulk lists. Automations that watch who engaged with a post or who followed a competitor produce smaller, hotter lists - that is what clients pay premiums for, and it uses the same meters.
  • Recruiting sourcing runs on the same pipeline. Candidate lists from Sales Navigator searches bill identically to agency lead-gen - recruiters were among the first heavy users.
  • The free plan is a workflow validator. 30 minutes a month proves a Phantom works before any card charge; the trial's 10-row export cap is the real limiter, so treat the trial as a field test, not a harvest.

The Honest Cons

  • LinkedIn risk sits with you. Phantoms run through your own account within public-data limits - no fake accounts, no bypassing - but volume spikes still get accounts flagged. Keep cadences human; no vendor removes platform risk.
  • Three meters, forecasted independently. More execution hours does not fix a slot shortage or empty email credits; the resources never convert into each other, and all reset monthly.
  • Credits burn on misses. Email lookups charge whether or not a match is found - bad targeting costs exactly the same as good targeting.
  • $69 is steep for occasional use. Solo prospectors below ~10 hours a month are paying for idle capacity; the 30-minute free plan is validation, not production.
  • Compliance posture, not certification. GDPR positioning and public-data-only tooling are documented; no SOC 2 or ISO certificate surfaced in official materials at check time.

Bottom Line

PhantomBuster is capacity you rent, not contacts you buy - price your workflow in hours and credits before committing and the plans make sense. Grow at $128 annual is the sweet spot for anyone selling prospecting as a service; Start proves the workflow; Scale is for agencies whose Phantoms never sleep. Respect the platform limits and the tool pays for itself; ignore them and you will be rebuilding your LinkedIn presence from scratch.

Questions we get asked

Will LinkedIn ban my account?

The risk is real and yours to manage. PhantomBuster runs automations through your own account on public data, with no fake accounts and no bypassing of platform safeguards, but no vendor can remove platform risk. Keep volumes conservative, warm up gradually, and prefer Sales Navigator automations - lower friction than basic LinkedIn. Sudden spikes in activity are what get accounts flagged.

What happens when I run out of execution time?

Automations pause until the monthly cycle resets or you upgrade - there are no automatic overage charges. That makes budgeting safe but means a busy first week can leave later weeks idle on Start's 20 hours. Email and AI credits work the same way, with no rollover.

Is the free plan usable for real work?

After the 14-day trial (2 execution hours total, 10-row export cap), the free plan keeps 1 slot and 30 minutes a month - enough to validate one workflow's fields and output shape, not to run production prospecting. Real work starts at Start ($69/mo, 20 hours).
