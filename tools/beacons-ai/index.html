What Beacons Actually Is (From Someone Running One)

I set up a Beacons page for my side project in early 2025, and it has quietly become the single page I send everyone to - the link in my TikTok bio, my YouTube about section, my email signature. Beacons is a link-in-bio page and creator storefront rolled into one: unlimited link buttons, a digital product store, a media kit, email collection, tip jars, and invoicing, all on one free page. Think Linktree, except Linktree stops at links while Beacons actually processes the payments.

The pitch is consolidation. Instead of paying separately for a link-in-bio tool, an email tool, a media kit builder, a storefront, and an appointment scheduler, Beacons bundles all of it. The free plan is genuinely usable, not a crippled demo: unlimited links, a working store, a tip jar, and email capture. The catch - and there is always a catch - is a 9% transaction fee on every sale while you are on Free or the $10 Creator plan.

How People Actually Make Money With It

Beacons is not a traffic source either - it is the conversion layer that sits between your social audience and your income. The monetization paths that work:

  • Selling digital products off a short-form audience. The classic 2026 playbook: post faceless TikTok or Reels content in a niche (budgeting templates, Notion setups, workout plans), put the Beacons link in bio, and sell a $9-29 product. Beacons handles checkout, file delivery, and even order bumps on paid plans. This is the same playbook people run on Stan Store or Whop - Beacons is the cheapest way in.
  • Brand deals through the media kit. The built-in media kit pulls your social stats and packages them into a pitchable page. If you have even 5k engaged followers, this alone is worth the page - brands ask for a media kit in the first email, every time.
  • AI brand outreach (Creator plan and up). Beacons generates pitch messages to brands based on your niche and stats. It will not replace a good cold email you write yourself, but it cuts the blank-page problem and most people send zero pitches because writing them feels hard.
  • Tips, memberships, and appointments. Tip jars monetize audiences that will not buy products. Paid plans add memberships (recurring revenue) and scheduling, which coaches and freelancers use to book paid calls straight from the bio link.
  • Client invoicing. Freelancers use Beacons' zero-fee invoicing to bill clients - a small thing, but it turns the page from a cost into a business tool.

The math that decides everything: the 9% fee versus the $30 Creator Plus plan. Break-even lands around $280-330/month in sales depending on billing cycle. Below that, stay on Free or Creator and eat the fee. Above it, Creator Plus pays for itself and then some. Stripe's processing fees apply on every plan - that is not Beacons' cut, that is the payment network's.

Beacons vs The Field

  • vs Stan Store ($29/mo flat, no transaction fee): Stan Store wins on fee simplicity for established digital sellers. Beacons wins for anyone starting from zero - a $0 entry point versus $29 before your first sale.
  • vs Linktree: Linktree is cleaner as a pure link list and slightly cheaper at entry ($5/mo), but its commerce features are thin. If your bio link is just links, Linktree is fine. If your bio link is supposed to earn money, Beacons does more.
  • vs Whop: Whop is stronger for community products and has a marketplace with discovery traffic. Beacons is stronger as a personal brand hub where everything - links, store, media kit - lives in one place.
  • vs Carrd ($19/yr): Carrd is the cheapest custom page you can buy, but there is no store, no payments, no media kit. Different job entirely.

Bottom Line

Beacons is the best free starting point in the creator monetization stack, and that is not faint praise - the free plan's store and media kit actually work. Two honest warnings: first, the 9% fee on lower plans is real money once sales move, so know your break-even and upgrade to Creator Plus when you cross roughly $300/month. Second, Beacons gives you the page, not the audience - if your social content does not bring clicks, no link-in-bio tool will fix that. Start free, validate that a product sells at all, then let the fee math tell you when to pay.

❓ FAQ

Is Beacons.ai actually free?

Yes - the free plan is permanent, not a trial. You get unlimited links, 5 bio pages, a basic media kit, a working store, and 50 email sends per month. The trade-offs are 9% transaction fees on every sale and visible 'Powered by Beacons' branding.

How do you make money with Beacons?

Beacons is the conversion layer, not the traffic source. The standard playbook: build a niche audience on TikTok, Reels, or YouTube, put your Beacons link in bio, and sell digital products ($9-29 templates, plans, guides) or land brand deals using the built-in media kit. Beacons handles checkout and delivery; your content brings the buyers.

When should I upgrade from the free plan?

Two triggers. First, branding: if brands or sponsors see your page, $10/mo for a custom domain and no Beacons logo is worth it immediately. Second, the fee math: Creator Plus at $30/mo drops the 9% transaction fee to 0%, which pays for itself around $300/month in sales. Below that, stay free and keep the money.
