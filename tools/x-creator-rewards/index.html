What X Monetization Actually Is (September 2026 Edition)

I enrolled in X's old monetization program in 2023. My first month paid $11.40 from ads shown under my replies. That program no longer exists. On August 8, 2026, X emailed every creator: Ads Revenue Sharing ends September 7, the final payment lands September 11, and a replacement called Original Content Rewards opens for applications September 8. If you are reading a guide that says "5 million impressions" or "ads in your replies," it describes a dead system.

The new program pays on qualified impressions of original content, judged by X. That sentence contains the three things that decide whether you earn anything: qualified, original, judged by X.

The Money Angle

Ranked by what actually pays most X creators, not by what X markets:

1. Brand deals and external funnels - most people's real income. A 50,000-follower account in a defined niche charges $200-2,000 per sponsored post. The same post's organic payout share is single-digit dollars. Serious X creators route traffic to a newsletter (Kit, beehiiv), a Gumroad product, affiliate links, or client work - and 80% or more of their income comes from outside the platform.

2. Creator Subscriptions - the reliable floor. Followers pay a monthly price you set for subscriber-only content. On web purchases X takes nothing until $50,000 in lifetime earnings, then 10% (in-app purchases get an extra app-store cut first). A hundred subscribers at $4.99 is about $500 a month - more than several million impressions of ad share ever paid under the old rates.

3. Original Content Rewards - the passive floor, and it is low. Old-program data, the only hard numbers we have: roughly $8.50 per million qualified impressions as a global average, about $29.75 for US audiences, $23.80 UK, $21.25 Canada, $5-10 for most of Asia. One creator's public first payout after crossing the old 5M threshold was $125.43. Around 90% of creators made under $100 a month. X has not published the new rate; the official line is that the pool roughly doubled after 2025 Premium growth. Treat every projection, including optimistic ones, as a guess until you have two payouts of your own.

4. Tips - rounding error. 0% platform cut, tiny volume.

The comparison that should set expectations: the same 1 million impressions paid $1,500-5,000 on long-form YouTube and $400-1,000 on TikTok in the same period. On X, expect $8-30. X pays coffee money; the audience it builds pays rent.

Where AI fits: the shape that works is AI as the speed layer and you as the substance layer. Draft with AI, then insert your own numbers, your own screenshots, your own failures - the things both the originality judgment and the audience check for. Pure AI batch production fails three ways at once: it rarely passes the originality bar, it trips the engagement-bait scanner, and it converts nobody into subscribers or buyers.

What a Qualified Impression Is

Four conditions, all required, any one failing means the view pays nothing:

  • The post must appear on a Premium subscriber's home timeline. Profile visits, search results, notifications, and reply placement do not count.
  • At least 50% of the post's content area must be visible on screen - feed-skimming past a post does not count.
  • Each viewer counts once per post, no matter how many times they scroll past it.
  • Only Premium subscribers count. Free-tier views are decoration.

This is why the same account can show 8 million raw impressions and a six-figure-adjacent payout, or 8 million raw impressions and $40. The denominator that matters is Premium reach, which is why niche choice - finance, tech, AI, business, where paying subscribers cluster - matters more than follower count.

The AI Content Rules

X's original content definition explicitly allows self-made images, illustrations, and memes, plus original reporting, analysis, articles, self-shot photos and video. Comments and reaction posts qualify if they add substantial value. Explicitly excluded: reposting other people's work, re-uploading content downloaded from other platforms, watermark-only changes, trivial text over an image, light image edits, and engagement-bait formats (ask-for-likes, follow-for-follow, reply-farming).

AI-specific rules: since March 2026, realistic AI-generated video - war and disaster footage especially - must be labeled, or the account is suspended from payouts for 90 days; repeat violations mean permanent removal from the program. Since July 2026, Grok scans for engagement-bait patterns. And one warning shot aimed at everyone: a proposed change to weight impressions by the creator's region was paused after Nigerian creators protested en masse - paused, not withdrawn. X retains the power to rewrite your payout formula with an email.

Bottom Line

X monetization in 2026 got easier to enter and much harder to game. The tenfold drop in the view threshold is real, and it favors exactly one kind of creator: someone with genuine opinions, data, or experience who uses AI to publish faster - not someone using AI to publish more. Expect the direct payout to stay small, use Creator Subscriptions and off-platform funnels for the real money, and pick a niche where Premium subscribers cluster. If you can clear 500 verified followers and 500K qualified views, you are already in the top few percent of accounts. That is worth $30 a month in ad share and a distribution channel worth several thousand - know which number you are playing for.

FAQ

Is the requirement 500 followers or 500 Premium followers?

500 verified followers - people who pay for X Premium themselves. Premium users are roughly 3-8% of the platform, so the practical bar is 3,000-10,000+ total followers. A large free-tier audience does not qualify on its own, while a small tech or finance audience can. Check your verified-follower count in Creator Studio before applying.

How much do I actually get paid per million impressions?

The new program's rate is unpublished. Under the old program, measured rates were about $8.50 per million qualified impressions globally, $29.75 for US audiences, $23.80 UK, $21.25 Canada, $5-10 across most of Asia - and around 90% of creators earned under $100 a month. The same million impressions paid $1,500-5,000 on long-form YouTube. X's direct payout is a floor, not an income; subscriptions, brand deals and funnels are where the money is.

Can AI-generated content earn under the new rules?

AI-assisted content can, AI-only content is a bad bet. The original content definition allows self-made images, illustrations and memes, original analysis and reporting, and valuable commentary - so AI drafting plus your own data, screenshots and opinions fits. Realistic AI-generated video must be labeled since March 2026, or you face 90 days out of the program and permanent removal on repeat. Reposted content, watermark-only edits, trivial text and engagement bait are explicitly excluded, and Grok has scanned for bait patterns since July 2026.
