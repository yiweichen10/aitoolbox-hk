Money Angle

  • Margin arbitrage is the whole business case. Route bulk workloads (summarization, classification, extraction, embeddings) to an open model at $0.30/$1.20 per 1M tokens and keep frontier APIs for the hard 10%. On a support-ticket product doing 50M tokens a month, that difference is rent money, not rounding error.
  • Fine-tune once, sell forever. A LoRA run on a small model costs single-digit dollars. Train it on a vertical corpus - medical intake phrasing, legal clause extraction, a brand's tone - and sell the fine-tuned endpoint as the product.
  • Burst GPUs beat buying GPUs. Video and batch-processing products rent dedicated H100s at $5.49/hr for launch week and switch them off after. The capex alternative is a $30K card gathering dust.
  • The free model covers demos and evals. Ternary Bonsai 27B at $0 means your public playground and regression tests cost nothing while paid models handle production.
  • Switching cost is one base URL. The API is OpenAI-compatible, so existing code points at it with a config change - the full comparison lives in best LLM inference pricing.

The Honest Cons

  • There is no single price. 200+ models each carry their own sheet; budgeting means actually reading tables instead of quoting one number.
  • No account-level free tier. One model is $0, everything else bills from the first token - plan a small top-up for development months.
  • Open models have real gaps on the hardest reasoning and long-context work; the honest architecture is a router, not a replacement. OpenRouter is the shopping mall if you want more model variety.
  • Capacity sells out. Several cluster SKUs on the pricing page read "Sold Out" at any given minute; reserved deals are sales conversations, not checkout clicks.
  • Fine-tuning minimums ($4-22 per job) punish tiny experiments on larger models.

Questions we get asked

How much cheaper is Together AI than frontier APIs?

On bulk workloads, dramatically: DeepSeek V4.1 Flash runs $0.30/$1.20 per 1M tokens and gpt-oss-120B $0.15/$0.60, while frontier flagship APIs charge multiples of that for the same output volume. The exact model-by-model math is tracked in our LLM API pricing comparison. The honest pattern is routing: cheap models for 90% of volume, frontier models for the hard 10%.

Is there a free tier or free credits?

No subscription and no account-level free tier appears on the official pricing page as of 2026-11-23. One model (Ternary Bonsai 27B) is listed at $0, which covers demos and evals; paid models bill from the first token, so plan a small top-up for development months.

Can I fine-tune a model and deploy it privately?

Yes - LoRA and full fine-tuning are priced per 1M training tokens (from $0.34 for small models, $4-22 minimum charge per job), and custom models deploy on dedicated single-tenant GPUs or reserved PTU capacity. Fine-tuning supports the usual data-privacy concerns for client work.
