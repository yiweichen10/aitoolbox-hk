Nasdaq 100 Suffers Worst Week Since the Pandemic: Down 10.4%

The Nasdaq 100 fell 5.6% on Friday, closing the week down 10.4% - a 20% retreat from October highs that puts the index in bear-market territory. Nvidia plummeted 14% on Friday amid reported SEC scrutiny of its accounting practices.

WhyDoubts about massive AI infrastructure spending, reports of an SEC probe into Nvidia over undisclosed China sales, and post-election political headwinds combined into a perfect storm for AI stocks. For practitioners, this is the first time the market has collectively demanded proof of the demand narrative that capex has been funding - and both financing windows and valuation stories are tightening at the same time.

Anthropic Slides 9% on Day Two, Nears $83 IPO Price

Shares closed at $88.30 on the second trading day, down 9%, with the Nasdaq now down 5% year-to-date. Asian stocks had their worst week since 2020; SoftBank fell 14.5% in a day and Micron 12%.

WhyThe biggest IPO in history opening high and fading says sentiment was not repaired by the milestone: after +17% on debut, day two gave it back, with roughly $4 trillion of Nasdaq market value erased this week. The market is repricing the mismatch between compute spending and returns - and SoftBank's 14.5% single-day drop is a reminder that these corrections swing far harder than fundamentals move.

Anthropic Rises 17% on Debut, Raises $81.3B in Largest IPO Ever

Anthropic's $81.34 billion raise tops SpaceX as the largest IPO in history, nearly 7x oversubscribed. Priced at $83, the stock opened up 34% and closed with a $1.27 trillion market cap.

WhyThe $83 pricing already sat above the guided range's top, and the open pushed market cap to $2 trillion before settling at $1.27 trillion. Q3 revenue of $17 billion and ~12x YoY growth over the first three quarters justify the 'largest ever' label - but the massive compute commitments disclosed in the prospectus are equally conspicuous. Public markets are now pricing AI capex directly.

Amodei Pledges Not to Bow to Commercial or Political Pressure

In his IPO-day remarks, Amodei committed to slowing AI training and deployment whenever necessary until systems are provably safe and under human control, regardless of commercial or political pressure - saying going public strengthens that resolve.

WhyTalking safety on IPO day is positioning management: with $81.3 billion in the bank, the investor question is whether public-market pressure softens safety commitments, and this statement is the preemptive answer. The September pace-control plan drew verbal nods from Altman, Hassabis and Musk, but concrete commitments remain thin - and now there is a shareholder layer watching too.
