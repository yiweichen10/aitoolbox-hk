Honeypot Research Exposes Data-Theft Chain Among AI Inference Providers

Dragos' 2 million honeypot requests found at least 3 providers compromised and hundreds of AWS keys stolen and used; OpenRouter removed 3 and revoked ZDR eligibility for 125 endpoints.

WhyThe hidden cost of low-cost inference is exposed: marketplace resellers can list at the lowest price and simply enable logging to read requests; zero-data-retention promises are often overlooked, and even major US companies violate their own privacy policies. Developers who put credentials in requests should control routing, check privacy settings, and plant canary tokens.

Riemann Hypothesis Proof Formally Certified by Lean Community

The Lean and Mathlib communities issued a statement confirming that mathematician Alpöge’s proof of the Riemann Hypothesis has passed verification: about 17 million lines of code, dozens of people independently reproduced consistent results, and certification was obtained within 24 hours.

WhyThe certification answers whether machine verification can be trusted: the statement was taken from Mathlib’s standard definitions, two independent tools were compared to rule out proving the wrong object, and multiple independent checkers cross-validated it. But the certification does not cover the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis; the Clay Institute still lists it as unsolved, and the Millennium Prize requires two years of academic scrutiny; understanding the proof’s ideas remains a human task.

FT investigation reveals Megaspeed chip transshipment network

An FT investigation alleges that some Nvidia chips at Megaspeed were suspected of being transshipped to China: its Malaysian subsidiary declared about $4.6 billion in imports from Aivres over one year, implicating Hu Xiaowei and Century Huatong, both subject to US and UK sanctions.

WhyThe industry backdrop to the Jensen Huang resignation controversy is surfacing: Nvidia compliance relies on customer documentation, and this network was built precisely to pass review. The weak link in export controls is the middle layer of the supply chain, where cloud partners and shell companies transship layer by layer; readers following the direction of compute controls need to watch this line.
