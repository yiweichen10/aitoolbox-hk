Grand Theft Auto VI sold $3.79B on day one without generative AI

Grand Theft Auto VI earned $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales and became the highest-rated game on Metacritic after a 13-year wait, according to TechCrunch. Rockstar built it without generative AI, with about 5,000 people working for more than a decade and 30,000 distinct nonplayable characters. Its launch coincided with Anthropic's record $81.3 billion IPO.

Why it matters

The comparison is less about games versus AI than about what gets counted as waste. Rockstar's repeated rework and delays produced detail that prompt-driven generation cannot easily replicate, while the industry's efficiency story rewards removing exactly that trial and error. For studios, the practical constraint is that generation can compress production without guaranteeing the depth that made this launch a cultural event.
