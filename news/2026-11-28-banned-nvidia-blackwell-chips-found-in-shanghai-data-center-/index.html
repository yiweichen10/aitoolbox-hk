Banned Nvidia Blackwell chips found in Shanghai data center, Bloomberg reports

Two Chinese companies installed Nvidia Blackwell chips at a Shanghai data center linked to Alice Huang, Bloomberg reports, citing regulatory filings. Shanghai Shuoyao pledged a GB300-NVL72 rack as collateral for a 96 million yuan ($14 million) loan; Shanghai Kezhan listed a GB200 server at the same site. Bloomberg traced the rack to Gigabyte through a serial number in a warranty database. Exporting the chips without a US license is banned.

Why it matters

The evidence here is unusually documentary: loan collateral records and a warranty database serial, not just intelligence estimates. That makes the finding harder for suppliers to wave away and raises the compliance bar for anyone financing or insuring high-end server racks. For export-control enforcement, the lesson is that secondary paperwork can expose hardware long after it leaves the factory.
