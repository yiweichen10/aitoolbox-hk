Anthropic Slides 9% on Day Two, Nears $83 IPO Price

Shares closed at $88.30 on the second trading day, down 9%, with the Nasdaq now down 5% year-to-date. Asian stocks had their worst week since 2020; SoftBank fell 14.5% in a day and Micron 12%.

Why it matters

The biggest IPO in history opening high and fading says sentiment was not repaired by the milestone: after +17% on debut, day two gave it back, with roughly $4 trillion of Nasdaq market value erased this week. The market is repricing the mismatch between compute spending and returns - and SoftBank's 14.5% single-day drop is a reminder that these corrections swing far harder than fundamentals move.
