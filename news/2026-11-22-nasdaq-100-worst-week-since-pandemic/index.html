Nasdaq 100 Suffers Worst Week Since the Pandemic: Down 10.4%

The Nasdaq 100 fell 5.6% on Friday, closing the week down 10.4% - a 20% retreat from October highs that puts the index in bear-market territory. Nvidia plummeted 14% on Friday amid reported SEC scrutiny of its accounting practices.

Why it matters

Doubts about massive AI infrastructure spending, reports of an SEC probe into Nvidia over undisclosed China sales, and post-election political headwinds combined into a perfect storm for AI stocks. For practitioners, this is the first time the market has collectively demanded proof of the demand narrative that capex has been funding - and both financing windows and valuation stories are tightening at the same time.
