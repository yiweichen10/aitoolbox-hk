MiniMax MIM Achieves First Practical Breaks on Multiple NIST Candidates

MiniMax's MIM delivered the first practical cryptanalysis breaks on several NIST candidate algorithms, solving 55 previously unsolved McEliece challenge instances - up to a 43,000x speedup over prior records.

Why it matters

Cryptanalysis is an ideal agent testbed: binary success, attack scripts either run or fail, no room for hallucination. MIM releases traces and proofs-of-concept but not the model itself - internal research models remain the moat. No need to panic about deployed standards, but NIST candidates falling one after another shows the safety margin is thinner than assumed.
