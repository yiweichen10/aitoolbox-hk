Runway's Software Factory: Agents Now Land 38% of Merged PRs

Runway reports agents drove 38% of merged PRs in October - up from 0.1% in June - with monthly merges going from 2 to over 3,000, and first-approval wait times down from 2.3 hours to 5 minutes.

Why it matters

Read the rollback-rate comparison carefully: AI approvals concentrate on low-risk changes, and on a like-for-like basis the article's own numbers show parity - the raw 0.10% vs 0.5% overstates the advantage. The engineering takeaway is moving from per-change human gatekeeping to risk-tiered gates: database migrations, secrets and auth stay human-locked. Their staged handover write-up is a copyable template for metrics and delegation order.
