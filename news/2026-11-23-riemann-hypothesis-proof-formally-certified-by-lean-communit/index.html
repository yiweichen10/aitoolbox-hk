Riemann Hypothesis Proof Formally Certified by Lean Community

The Lean and Mathlib communities issued a statement confirming that mathematician Alpöge’s proof of the Riemann Hypothesis has passed verification: about 17 million lines of code, dozens of people independently reproduced consistent results, and certification was obtained within 24 hours.

Why it matters

The certification answers whether machine verification can be trusted: the statement was taken from Mathlib’s standard definitions, two independent tools were compared to rule out proving the wrong object, and multiple independent checkers cross-validated it. But the certification does not cover the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis; the Clay Institute still lists it as unsolved, and the Millennium Prize requires two years of academic scrutiny; understanding the proof’s ideas remains a human task.
