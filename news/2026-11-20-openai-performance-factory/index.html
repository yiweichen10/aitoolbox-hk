OpenAI Uses Codex to Auto-Investigate Performance Regressions

OpenAI's Performance Factory has Codex agents investigate and fix ChatGPT performance regressions: 1,700+ investigations and 600+ root causes over three months, with 70% spot-check accuracy.

Why it matters

Regression triage just moved from human piling to an agent factory: alerts open tasks, independent sessions adversarially review diagnoses, and human engineers still merge fix PRs. The 70% spot-check accuracy says automated localization is usable but not fully autonomous - and OpenAI is already exploring auto-ship for low-risk fixes. SRE and reliability teams should watch this pipeline closely.
