Tencent Hunyuan opens Hy4 flash weights

Tencent Hunyuan opens Hy4 flash weights: 224B total parameters, 12B activated, 1M context, multimodal; API pricing is RMB 0.5 per million input tokens and RMB 2 per million output tokens.

Why it matters

It is self-distilled from the flagship Hy4 rather than trained from scratch, a compute-saving route that pushes capabilities down into a cost-effective tier; KV cache is only 1/6 of Hy4 and decoding exceeds 150 tps. On launch day it gained Day 0 adaptation for Muxi domestic GPUs; for China's compute ecosystem, demand for privatizable weights is more practical than leaderboard rankings.
