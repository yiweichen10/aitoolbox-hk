AA Evaluation: GPT-6.2 More Concise but Less Transparent

AA personality benchmark: both GPT-6.2 models give answers 34–40% shorter, admitting uncertainty falls to 24 and 51, and transparency at 41 and 57 trails Claude's 81 and 90.

Why it matters

AA-PersonalityBench measures communication style, not accuracy; GPT-6.2 trades style for directness: less preamble, faster answers, but no longer volunteering uncertainty. Scores are relative percentiles — lower transparency doesn't mean a higher chance of answering incorrectly.
