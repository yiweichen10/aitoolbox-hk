Anthropic Mathematician Claims Claude Proof of the Riemann Hypothesis

Mathematician Alpöge says the claimed proof also covers the generalized Riemann hypothesis, published as 14 Lean packages spanning over 250,000 printed pages. Hours later, Stanford released a competing human-only draft by mathematician Masullo.

Why it matters

Lean formalization makes a quarter-million-page proof machine-checkable line by line for the first time - but whether the problem statement was correctly translated into code still relies on expert spot-checks, and that will remain the long-running controversy around AI mathematical proofs. Meanwhile, the human draft had its questionable points found by other AI-assisted reviewers within eight hours. The two routes checking each other is more notable than who proved it first.
