Amodei Pledges Not to Bow to Commercial or Political Pressure

In his IPO-day remarks, Amodei committed to slowing AI training and deployment whenever necessary until systems are provably safe and under human control, regardless of commercial or political pressure - saying going public strengthens that resolve.

Why it matters

Talking safety on IPO day is positioning management: with $81.3 billion in the bank, the investor question is whether public-market pressure softens safety commitments, and this statement is the preemptive answer. The September pace-control plan drew verbal nods from Altman, Hassabis and Musk, but concrete commitments remain thin - and now there is a shareholder layer watching too.
