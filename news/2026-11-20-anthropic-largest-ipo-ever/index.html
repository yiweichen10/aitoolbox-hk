Anthropic Rises 17% on Debut, Raises $81.3B in Largest IPO Ever

Anthropic's $81.34 billion raise tops SpaceX as the largest IPO in history, nearly 7x oversubscribed. Priced at $83, the stock opened up 34% and closed with a $1.27 trillion market cap.

Why it matters

The $83 pricing already sat above the guided range's top, and the open pushed market cap to $2 trillion before settling at $1.27 trillion. Q3 revenue of $17 billion and ~12x YoY growth over the first three quarters justify the 'largest ever' label - but the massive compute commitments disclosed in the prospectus are equally conspicuous. Public markets are now pricing AI capex directly.
