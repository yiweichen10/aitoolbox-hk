Thinking Machines Lab Releases Laptop-Runnable Inkling 2 Mini

A recurrent transformer whose middle 12 layers run 3 times per token. MoE total parameters 26.4B with 3.4B active, around 20GB - it runs on a 32GB Mac.

Why it matters

The recurrent architecture hides serial depth inside layer loops, so reasoning no longer has to be written out fully in chain-of-thought - which turns CoT monitorability into a fresh research question, exactly why TML open-sourced it alongside the variable-loop variants. Native NVFP4 quantization and the 20GB footprint pull the lightweight-model competition down to consumer hardware.
